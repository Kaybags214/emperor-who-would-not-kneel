# Character — Borchu

> Added during this consistency pass — Borchu recurs across Chapters 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, and 12 (one of the most present recurring characters in the arc) with no character sheet anywhere. Pulled directly from the text, not invented.

## Role
An old soldier among Egris's earliest followers. The camp's second voice of authority after Egris himself.

## Personality
Blunt, unbowed, dryly observant. He's introduced specifically *not* kneeling when Seraphel's army first descends — "That was why Egris tolerated him." He says the things others are too afraid to: calling out Egris's growing authority to his face ("They follow you. They wait for you to speak before they move. If that is not ownership, what is it?"), and later counting his refusals out loud ("That is the third time you have refused power, Egris... How many more times will you be given the choice before someone stops asking?").

## Function in the Story
Drills the camp's defenses, stands watch, and is usually the first to physically position himself beside Egris when a threat arrives (Ch.9, Ch.11). He is loyalty that argues back — proof, alongside Suro, that Egris's followers aren't yes-men.

## Key Relationship
**Egris:** The two men respect each other precisely because Borchu never bows. He is the closest thing the camp has to an equal for Egris to talk to, rather than lead.

## Visual Notes (from the text)
A wound splitting the left side of his face — a memory of how he died, bloodless like all wounds in the Wastes. Carries a broken weapon (initially a length of sharpened stone) into most fights.
