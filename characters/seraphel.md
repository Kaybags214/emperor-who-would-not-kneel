# Character — Seraphel

> Added during this consistency pass — Seraphel is God's on-page envoy and the central figure of Chapters 5–6, yet had no character sheet despite `characters/god.md` existing. Pulled directly from the text, not invented.

## Role
General of the Seventh Host. Voice of the Throne. God's envoy sent to reclaim Egris — first by argument, then by force.

## What He Represents
Heaven's case made in person: not tyranny, but a sincere belief that Egris is wasting real strength by refusing the one structure that could make it permanent. He shows Egris a vision of a "world at peace" — a golden city with no fear, no hunger, no war — and it costs those in it their choice. Egris's read: *"They are spared confusion." / "They are spared choice."*

## Personality
Formal, controlled, genuinely trying to persuade rather than simply conquer. He escalates to force only when persuasion fails, and he is capable of being moved: watching Egris refuse both thrones in the same day, and watching the camp choose to stand rather than be commanded, visibly changes something in him.

## Arc Across Ch.5–6
Descends to command submission → argues instead → summons Heaven's full host when Egris still refuses → and ultimately withdraws the entire host without a single blow struck, once Satan's rival plot forces the question and Egris still refuses both offers. His last exchange is with a nameless young soldier he singles out — *"Choose carefully." "I did."* — suggesting he leaves having actually listened.

## Key Relationship
**Egris:** Adversarial but not hateful. Closer to two generals who respect each other's discipline than to enemies. Unresolved whether he returns in Arc 2.

## Visual Notes (from the text)
Bright, unscratched metal armor fitted like a second skin; a cloak of white feathers that moves without wind; a burning sword sheathed at his side. Where his feet touch ground, small white flowers rise. His face is "almost human — too still, too perfect," with no reflection in his eyes.
