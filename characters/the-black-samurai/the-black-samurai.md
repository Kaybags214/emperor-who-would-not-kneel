# Character Bible — The Black Samurai

> **Status:** Draft v1. Working name and details below are a starting point — swap anything that doesn't feel right. This character can live inside *The Emperor Who Would Not Kneel* or anchor his own series; the design is built to do either.

---

## Working Name

**Adé** — Yoruba, meaning *crown / royalty*.

Chosen deliberately: it ties the character to the crown motif at the heart of this project (the crown driven into the black stone between the two Gates), while standing on its own. Full name proposal: **Adébáyò**, shortened to **Adé**. Epithet used by others: **"the Masterless"** or **"the Kneeless Blade."**

*(Not attached to the name? Alternatives that carry the same weight: Kofi, Sekou, Jabari, Oba.)*

---

## Logline

A masterless swordsman with no lord, no country, and no god left to serve — carrying a blade sharper than his memory of who he used to be, walking toward the one man who took everything, refusing to kneel the entire way.

---

## One-Look Identity

- Bold natural Afro, iconic in silhouette — you should know him from a rooftop away.
- Rich dark skin, rendered with dignity and full detail through the ink and cross-hatching — never a caricature, never a token.
- Weathered kimono/hakama over layered warrior garb; a single katana that has clearly been used, not displayed.
- Carries himself with a dangerous stillness — authority in posture, calm intensity in the eyes.

---

## Core Concept

Adé is **composure as a weapon**. Where others posture, he is quiet. He does not raise his voice, does not threaten, does not perform his strength — and that is exactly what makes him terrifying. He has already decided he will not bow, and everyone who meets him can feel that decision like a wall.

He is the answer to a simple question: *what does a man become when he refuses every master offered to him — lords, gods, and death itself — and keeps walking anyway?*

The Blackness of the character is **authentic and central**, not decorative and not the whole of him. He is a fully realized person first: his heritage, his history, and his features are drawn with pride and specificity, and the story lets him be a philosopher, a warrior, and a human being — not a symbol.

---

## Personality

- **Quiet, not cold.** He listens more than he speaks. When he does speak, it lands.
- **Unbought.** Money, titles, and salvation have all been offered. He has refused all three.
- **Disciplined grief.** He has lost enormous things and does not pretend otherwise, but he does not let it run him. It sharpens him.
- **Dry humor** that surfaces only with the very few people he trusts.
- **A hard code.** He will not kneel, will not beg, and will not abandon someone under his protection — even when it costs him.

---

## Backstory (skeleton — expand as you like)

Adé was raised a warrior in a proud homeland that was betrayed and broken by a power that promised protection. He served a lord, believed in something, and watched all of it burn on the word of men who called it "order." He buried the people he loved with his own hands.

Since then he has been **masterless by choice** — a ronin in the truest sense. He refuses to trade one collar for another. He is on a long road toward the man (or the power) responsible, and along the way he keeps encountering people the world has also thrown away. He doesn't set out to save them. He just doesn't look away.

---

## The Sword & Fighting Style

- **One blade, one purpose.** He is not a flashy fighter. His style is economy — minimal motion, maximum consequence. A single draw can end a duel.
- **Iaijutsu-forward:** the fight is often decided in the instant the blade leaves the sheath.
- **He never draws to intimidate.** If the sword comes out, someone is going down. This makes every unsheathing a moment of dread on the page.
- Visual signature for action pages: **stillness, then a single black slash of ink across the panel** — the aftermath shown before the motion.

---

## Themes He Carries

- **Refusal as identity** — the same spine as *The Emperor Who Would Not Kneel*. Adé is the sword-side answer to Genghis Khan's throne-side defiance.
- **Dignity under a world built to erase you** — his features, his people, and his history are drawn with full weight precisely because the world keeps trying to reduce him.
- **Freedom vs. belonging** — the cost of having no master is having no home. The story does not pretend that cost is small.

---

## Visual Direction (for artists)

- **Style:** dark seinen black-and-white manga ink — heavy blacks, textured hatching, dramatic contrast. Same aesthetic family as the rest of this project (*Berserk* / *Vagabond* / *Afro Samurai* energy).
- **Skin:** rendered through inking and hatching with care and dignity — depth and richness, never flattened, never caricatured.
- **Hair:** the Afro is iconic and integrated into the samurai silhouette — his defining shape.
- **Wardrobe:** traditional Japanese samurai garb (kimono, hakama, layered warrior wear) that is worn, weathered, and clearly battle-used — blended with his own edge.
- **Pose language:** composed, grounded, dangerous calm. Authority through stillness. Intensity lives in the eyes, not in a snarl.
- **Atmosphere:** stark, cinematic, lone-warrior mood. Lots of negative space around him.

---

## Story Hooks (seeds for later)

1. **The road to the one who did it.** A revenge spine — but the target may turn out to be a system, a god, or a version of himself, not just a man.
2. **The ones he can't leave behind.** A recurring pattern of taking in the discarded, against his own stated rule.
3. **The offer.** At some point a power (a lord, a god, the Reaper) offers him everything back if he will just kneel once. The whole character is built for that single panel.
