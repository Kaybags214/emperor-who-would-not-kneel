# Character — Satan

## Role
Antagonist / Temptation. The supreme force of individual will in the afterlife.

## What Satan Represents
Rebellion, desire, individual will, and the destruction of imposed authority. He was the first being to refuse God's order. He considers this his greatest achievement.

## Portrayal
Satan is more charismatic than God. His offer feels more honest, more personal, more aligned with who Egris actually is. This makes him more dangerous.

He claims to be Egris's natural ally because Egris refuses to kneel. But Satan's freedom has a hidden cost: he wants every person to serve their own desires, even if that destroys loyalty, love, and civilization. His "freedom" is not liberation — it is a different kind of chain.

## How He Sees Egris
A soul who already did what Satan did — refused the throne above him and built his own. He sees Egris as a mirror of himself. He is wrong about this. He cannot understand why Egris cares about the people who follow him.

## His Offer
An army of demons and the power to conquer every world beyond death. Limitless conquest. No army that can ever defeat him again.

## His Flaw
He cannot build. He can only tear down and promise that what replaces the ruins will be better. He has never actually delivered on that promise.

The arc where he sends Egris's former rival as an assassin — offering the rival revenge — reveals Satan's true method: he does not unify. He weaponizes grievances. His "empire" is an army of personal vendettas with no common purpose.

## Power Type
**Fear and Temptation** — Satan's power comes from desire, specifically the desire for things people cannot have or are forbidden from wanting. He does not require worship — only hunger.

## Visual Notes
- Should not look monstrous. More compelling than God visually — sharper, more human-feeling, easier to look at.
- The danger is that he looks trustworthy. He should look like someone who has been genuinely wronged and is completely reasonable about it.
- No obvious evil tells. Let the reader figure it out before Egris does.
