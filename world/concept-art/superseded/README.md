# Superseded Concept Art

**the-crown-in-the-stone.png** — a finished splash illustration of Egris driving a crown into the black stone between the two gates as a territorial claim.

**Status: not canon.** Confirmed by the author — the finished Chapter 2 script (both the original prose draft and its later script conversion) has Egris shove both offered crowns to the ground and simply walk away, with no planting. This image predates that version of the scene and is kept here for reference/history only, not as a visual guide for illustrating Chapter 2.
