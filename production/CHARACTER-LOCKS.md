## EGRIS — protagonist 🔒

- **Name is `Egris`.** Not Khan, not Genghis Khan, not Temujin. The one remaining "Genghis
  Khan" string in the repo is a deliberate comp in `PUBLISHING-GUIDE.md` ("a Genghis
  Khan-caliber emperor") — a pitch comparison, not his name.
- 🔒 **Core strength:** unites enemies by showing them a future larger than their grudges.
  He converts rivals; he does not grind them down.
- 🔒 **Core flaw:** he instinctively turns every relationship into a hierarchy. He cannot
  easily see followers as equals.
- 🔒 **Philosophy — do not modify, soften, or resolve.** He refuses Heaven's order and Hell's
  freedom-without-structure alike, and he refuses command over the people who follow him. When
  asked in Ch.6 whether accepting power would mean they must obey him, he answers **no**, and
  means it. When asked in Ch.8 what comes next, his honest answer is **"I do not know."** That
  uncertainty is the character. Do not write him a confident answer.
- 🔒 He is **not** a reluctant hero learning to lead. He knows exactly how to build the empire
  he is offered — that is precisely why he won't.
- 🔒 **No crown.** Ever, in Arc 1. Not carried, not worn, not implied.
- 🔒 **Weapon:** a cracked spear, which he plants in the ground (visually rhymed with the
  Rival's glaive).
- 🔒 **The Mark is on his LEFT hand/arm.** See `POWER-SYSTEM-LOCKS.md`.
- **Visual:** weathered not old; authority in posture, not expression; practical worn armour,
  nothing ornamental; plain gray robe on the black plain in Ch.1–2.

⚠ **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED — ethnic identity.** Story canon reads Mongol throughout
(`art-notes/visual-direction.md`: *"Mongol features. Not westernized."*; Ch.2 *"You brought
order to the chaos of the steppes."*; Ch.3 Samarkand; the concept sheet is lettered "GENGHIS
KHAN"). `PUBLISHING-GUIDE.md` calls him Black in three places (lines 23, 73, 249).
**Unresolved. Do not generate character art for Egris until the author rules.** Note also that
`characters/egris/egris-character-sheet.png` has the words "GENGHIS KHAN" lettered into the
image itself — the file cannot be corrected by text edit and will need re-lettering whichever
way this lands.

## THE REAPER 🔒

- 🔒 **She. An ancient Black woman.** Not a skeleton, no scythe, no glowing aura, no hood-and-
  bones iconography. The earlier "he" version is archived and non-canon.
- 🔒 **No personal name.** She is never given one and never offers one.
- 🔒 **Two forms, one entity:** *the Woman* (her chosen presence) and *the Absence* (her true
  nature — light-absorbing silhouette, unclear scale, sparse linework).
  **In Arc 1 she appears ONLY as the Woman.** The Absence is bible/design material and has no
  on-page appearance yet. Do not render it in Arc 1 production.
- 🔒 **She never asks anyone to kneel.** She makes no demand of Egris at any point. This is the
  whole point of her — the one power that wanted nothing from him.
- 🔒 **Never draw her in motion.** Everything around her moves — ash, cloaks, hair. She and her
  locs do not. She crosses the whole Wastes in a single page-turn (Ch.7 p.20→21) with no
  depicted movement.
- 🔒 **Spot colour:** pure B&W ink with ONE restrained near-black crimson accent — robe edges,
  her blood, the eye-ring. Never a wash.
- 🔒 **Ch.12 is not a rescue.** *"I am not saving you. I am giving you enough rope to see
  whether you hang yourself or build a ladder."* She pauses the Mark out of curiosity, not
  mercy, and says so. Her price stands: *"I will take what is owed."*
- 🔒 Her Ch.12 closer: *"Certainty is the first step toward tyranny."*

⚠ **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED — her eyes.** The scripts say **"snake-slit pupils"** (Ch.7 p.22
"Narrow, snake-slit pupils"; Ch.12 p.8 "Snake-slit pupils watching Egris"; Ch.12 p.13 "tilts
her head, snake-eyes unblinking"). Her bible says her eyes in the Woman form are **dark and
human** until a rare reveal, and the reveal is **darkened whites plus a muted blood-red ring
around the iris**. Her finished concept sheet draws exactly that — round human pupils, crimson
ring in the "EYES (REVEAL)" panel, no slit anywhere. **Three scripts vs. bible + finished art.
Unresolved. Do not render the Reaper's eyes until the author rules.**

## GOD ("the white power") 🔒

- 🔒 Not a bearded man in robes. **Light arranged into architecture** — geometric, vast, the
  suggestion of a figure. Cold, not warm. Beautiful the way a cathedral is beautiful.
- 🔒 **Not evil, and not to be written as evil.** He sincerely believes a being of Egris's
  influence operating outside divine law will destroy the people who follow him.
- 🔒 His flaw: he cannot conceive of voluntary loyalty that never needs enforcement.
- 🔒 When angry, he does not raise his voice — the world's laws bend.
- 🔒 Lettering: clean, borderless, weightless.
- Offer: a place among the heavenly hosts as a **general**, not a servant; one eternal law.

## SATAN ("the dark power") 🔒

- 🔒 **More human-looking than God, and this is the danger.** Sharp features, a small genuine
  smile, sits when others stand, moves to eye level. The reader should like him first.
- 🔒 **No obvious evil tells early.** Do not add horns, monstrousness, or menace cues.
- 🔒 His flaw: he cannot build. He weaponises grievances — his "empire" is an army of personal
  vendettas with no common purpose. The Former Rival is the proof.
- 🔒 Lettering: heavy, cracked balloons.
- Offer (Ch.6, the iron crown): *"an army that never doubts, never fears, and never asks
  whether your cause is worth dying for."*

## SURO, THE BLIND BLADE 🔒

- 🔒 A Black man, lean and precise; age indeterminate. Faded dark cloth over his eyes.
  **Blindness is never played as tragedy** — no stumbling, no groping, no pity. He reads the
  world by sound, vibration and breath, and navigates the Wastes better than sighted men.
- 🔒 **He does not strike first.** Hard rule. Iaijutsu-forward — the draw *is* the technique.
- 🔒 **The held breath** is his signature: stillness, not speed. No motion blur, no speed
  lines. Silence, then one decisive line of ink.
- 🔒 **He takes no side.** He cuts at Egris and at the Rival equally (Ch.7).
- 🔒 Introduced in **Ch.6** as `STRANGER`, having sat unnoticed at the camp's edge the whole
  chapter; named at the reveal. He stands exactly once in Ch.7 (p.14) — his standing is an event.
- ⚠ **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED** on story hook #4 in his bible (a "crossing paths with Egris"
  future hook, when they met in Ch.6). Flagged in place.

## ARGHUN 🔒
First follower. A **scribe, not a soldier** — 22 years in Samarkand recording Egris's tax
decrees and execution lists. Follows out of curiosity and a scribe's instinct, not loyalty or
worship. The camp's record-keeper and quiet conscience; asks the plain question everyone else
avoids. 🔒 The irony is never stated aloud: the first man to freely choose Egris is a man he
once ruled by fear. Visual notes in his bible are marked *inferred* — not established canon.

## BORCHU 🔒
Old soldier. 🔒 **Introduced specifically by NOT kneeling** when Seraphel's host descends —
*"That was why Egris tolerated him."* Loyalty that argues back: challenges Egris's growing
authority to his face and counts his refusals out loud. Usually the first to physically
position himself beside Egris (Ch.9, Ch.11). A wound splits the left side of his face;
carries a broken weapon.

## SERAPHEL 🔒
General of the Seventh Host, Voice of the Throne. 🔒 **Persuades before he threatens, and is
capable of being moved** — he withdraws Heaven's entire host in Ch.6 **without a single blow
struck**. Not a thug in armour. Visual: unscratched armour fitted like a second skin; a cloak
of white feathers moving without wind; a burning sheathed sword; small white flowers rise
where he steps; a face "almost human — too still, too perfect," with no reflection in his eyes.
Unresolved whether he returns in Arc 2.

## THE FORMER RIVAL 🔒
- 🔒 **He has no name and is never given one.** Credited only as "the Former Rival." This is
  deliberate. **Do not name him.**
- 🔒 The arc's real antagonist, and **the argument for structure made by someone with standing
  to make it** — he has watched real chaos in the Wastes. *"Structure is the only mercy the
  weak ever actually receive."* Do not flatten him into a villain.
- 🔒 He counts the dead and "would prefer fewer of them."
- 🔒 His Ch.7 gut-punch, which Egris cannot answer: *"You always thought your restraint made
  you clean. It didn't. It just made the cost land on other people instead of on you."*
- Visual: shaved head, single thin braid, scar across brow and nose bridge, repaired lamellar
  armour, divided commander's cloak, a glaive he plants like Egris plants his spear.
- Escalation: argue (Ch.7–8) → echo army (Ch.9–10) → colossus (Ch.11) → a real army (Ch.12).

## THE HOLLOW MEN 🔒
Souls whose features have been **partially erased**. 🔒 Not monsters — formerly people. The
horror is in what is missing, not what is added. Erase memory and identity on touch. 🔒 They
are driven off by **will**, not violence (Ch.4).

## THE ECHO WARRIORS 🔒
🔒 **Thirty** warrior-souls from a dozen eras, pale blue fire, perfect formation, bound by duty
that outlived death. Led by a **Roman Centurion** who salutes — an offer, not an attack. 🔒
They are refused with the truth, not defeated: their wars are over and they owe obedience to
no one, Egris included. They **release**; they do not die (Ch.10 p.13).
