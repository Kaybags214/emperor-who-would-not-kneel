# VISUAL STYLE BIBLE

> Consolidated art direction for AI-assisted production. Source: `art-notes/visual-direction.md`,
> the character bibles' "Visual Direction" sections, and the chapters' own SCRIPT NOTES, which
> outrank both. `🔒` = locked. `⚠` = open.

---

## House Style 🔒

- 🔒 **Black-and-white traditional Japanese manga ink.** Heavy blacks, fine hatching, screentone,
  strong negative space. **Seinen, not shonen** — *Berserk* / *Vagabond*, with *Afro Samurai*
  energy in the sword-side designs.
- 🔒 **Right-to-left.** Panel 1 sits at the **top-right** of every page.
- 🔒 **Dark. Heavy. Epic in scale, intimate in character moments.**
- 🔒 **The only colour permitted anywhere is the Reaper's accent:** one restrained near-black
  crimson, on robe edges, her blood, and the eye-ring in a reveal panel. **Never a wash.**
  Everything else in the series is pure B&W ink.

---

## Panel Language 🔒

- 🔒 Wide establishing shots for the territories — emphasise scale.
- 🔒 Tight close-ups for character beats. **Egris's eyes carry half of every chapter.**
- 🔒 **When two divine powers share a panel: asymmetrical layout.** They never get equal space.
  Let the composition fight.
- 🔒 **When Egris stands between them, he gets the centre panel. Always.** His placement is
  never accidental.
- 🔒 **At least one full silence page per chapter.** No dialogue. Let the art breathe.
- 🔒 **Near the Reaper, everything else loses detail.** Other figures in her panel are drawn
  with *less* line, not more. Her presence drains focus out of the frame.
- 🔒 Page-turn reveals are engineered. Keep the page you turn *from* quiet so the turn lands.

## Character Rendering 🔒

**EGRIS** — weathered, not old. Authority in posture, not expression. Practical worn armour,
nothing ornamental; a man who spent sixty years in the field. 🔒 **No crown, ever, in Arc 1.**
🔒 The Mark: a black vein from the left palm up the wrist and forearm — *not* glowing, *not*
magical-looking. Just wrong, like something leaking through.
⚠ **Ethnicity is unresolved — see `CHARACTER-LOCKS.md`. Do not generate Egris character art
until the author rules.**

**GOD** — 🔒 not a bearded man in robes. Light arranged into architecture: geometric, vast, the
*suggestion* of a figure. Cold, not warm. Beautiful the way a cathedral is beautiful. When
angry, the world's laws bend — he does not raise his voice.
Reference: `characters/god/god-character-sheet.png`.

**SATAN** — 🔒 more human-looking than God, and that is the danger. Sharp features, a small
genuine smile, closer to eye level, sits when others stand. 🔒 **No evil tells.** He should look
like someone who was genuinely wronged and is completely reasonable about it.
Reference: `characters/satan/satan-sample-page.png`.

**THE REAPER** — 🔒 tall ancient Black woman, deep brown-black skin in fine hatching and ink
wash, full dignity and detail. Long locs drifting to their own gravity. Severe dark ceremonial
robes, no ornament, crimson bleeding at the edges. Elegant deliberate hands. 🔒 **No skull, no
scythe, no aura, no theatrics.** 🔒 **Never draw her mid-motion** and never mid-transition
between forms except at a deliberate reveal beat. 🔒 **Arc 1 = the Woman form only**; the
Absence has no on-page appearance yet.
⚠ **Eyes unresolved** — scripts say snake-slit pupils, bible and art say human with a crimson
reveal ring. **Do not render her eyes until the author rules.**
Reference: `characters/the-reaper/the-reaper-character-sheet.png`.

**SURO** — 🔒 Black man, lean and precise, faded dark cloth over the eyes, **composed, never
tragic**. Light-toned open coat/haori over layered wraps — deliberately lighter than the
armoured cast so he reads as air against the gray. One plain sword. 🔒 **Posture is everything:**
balanced weight, feet reading the ground, head tilted to listen. Two signature poses: standing
and listening; mid-draw, blade half out.
Reference: `characters/suro-the-blind-blade/suro-character-sheet.png`.

**SERAPHEL** — unscratched armour fitted like a second skin; a cloak of white feathers that
moves without wind; a burning sword, sheathed. Small white flowers rise where he steps. 🔒 A
face "almost human — too still, too perfect," with **no reflection in his eyes**.

**THE FORMER RIVAL** — shaved head, single thin braid, scar across brow and nose bridge.
Repaired lamellar — cracked plates, re-laced belts. A divided commander's cloak that lifts like
a torn banner. 🔒 His planted glaive is framed at matching height with Egris's planted spear:
**two equals, not hero and villain.**
Reference: `characters/the-former-rival-character-sheet.png`.

**ARGHUN** — thin, stooped, cracked spectacles, ink-stained fingers (visible whenever his hands
are on panel), tattered court silk, heavy salvaged satchel he never puts down. Nervous but not
cowardly: flinches and holds ground, trembles and finishes his sentences. *No concept art exists.*

**BORCHU** — old soldier; a wound splitting the **left** side of his face, bloodless like all
wounds in the Wastes; a broken weapon. *No concept art exists.*

**THE HOLLOW MEN** — 🔒 souls whose features are **partially erased**. Not monsters — formerly
people. **The horror is in what is missing, not what is present.** *No concept art exists.*

**THE ECHO WARRIORS** — thirty translucent souls lit by pale blue fire, in perfect formation,
each from a different era and culture (Roman centurion, medieval knight, samurai, Mongol…).
🔒 United by **purpose, not origin**. *No concept art exists.*

**THE COLOSSUS** — twenty feet of ash, bone and ruin pressed into the vague shape of a man; a
broken helm for a head; arms of calcified stone and fused rusted weapons; a void at the chest
that pulls light in. 🔒 **Not demonic, not holy — just WRONG.** The Wastes given terrible
purpose. *No concept art exists.*

## Environments 🔒

**THE BORDER PLAIN** — black stone, flat, endless, polished like a blade. Featureless but for
the two gates and thousands of souls. Sky neither day nor night; gray, lightless; 🔒 **no horizon
line.** White gate ornate with light bleeding through the seams; black gate jagged and always
slightly open. Reference: `world/concept-art/the-border-gates.png`.

**HEAVEN** — vast, white, geometric. Cathedrals with no ceilings, light with no source.
Everything clean and proportional, overwhelming in its perfection. 🔒 The longer you look, the
more unsettling it becomes.

**HELL** — shifting, volcanic, loud, alive. Fortresses built while others burn; cities that
change layout between pages. The one constant: the Pit of First Rebellion, old stone, unchanged.

**THE WASTES** — ruins of every civilisation layered on each other; a Roman aqueduct crumbling
beside a Mesopotamian ziggurat. Gray sky, drifting ash, wind that sounds like voices. Ground
that crunches like dry cartilage. Mist that moves like a living thing. Broken pillars on the
horizon.

## Key Visual Moments to Protect 🔒

Verified against the finished scripts — the page numbers below are correct.

1. 🔒 **Ch.2, p.9 splash — the grip.** Egris takes both powers by the wrist and shoves both
   crowns to the stone. The first time either has ever been physically held. **Draw their
   shock as stillness, not flailing.** Keep p.8's last panel tight and quiet so the turn lands.
2. 🔒 **Ch.2, p.12 — the gates closing behind him.** This is the claim. He is not exiled; he is
   *leaving*. **Never show his face on this page — back turned only.**
3. 🔒 **Ch.3, p.11 splash** — two small figures, huge environment, footprints behind them: the
   first mark anyone has made in the Wastes *by choice*.
4. 🔒 **Ch.6 — Suro's reveal.** He has been in frame, unnoticed, the whole chapter. Place him
   where it makes sense in hindsight (edge of camp, still, silent) and put **visible ash on his
   shoulders** to sell how long he has been there.
5. 🔒 **Ch.7, p.21 splash — the Reaper's first full appearance.** She crosses the whole Wastes
   between pages. Total stillness in her and her locs while ash and cloaks move around her. A
   single drop of near-black crimson at her palm is **the only motion in the panel**.
6. 🔒 **Ch.7, p.14 — Suro stands.** The only time he stands in the chapter. Save it.
7. 🔒 **The Mark across the arc** — Ch.7 pain, Ch.8 darker, Ch.10 past the wrist, Ch.12 the whole
   forearm. **Never glow it.** The escalation is the storytelling, not any one panel.
8. 🔒 **Ch.10, p.13 splash — the Echo Warriors dissolve.** They **release**, they do not die.
   Soft screentone transitions, dissolving linework, the Centurion's eyes on Egris to the last.
9. 🔒 **Ch.11, p.13 — the ragged line forms.** Deliberately contrast it against the Echo
   Warriors' perfect formation in Ch.9. **These people are not soldiers.** They are individuals
   who decided to stand together.
10. 🔒 **Ch.11, p.27 — the colossus collapses.** It does not die in battle; it falls apart
    because the Rival's will breaks. **Crumbling, not exploding.**
11. 🔒 **Ch.12, p.18 — the Reaper touches the Mark.** It flares violently, then stops. Still
    there, still dangerous, frozen at the forearm.

## Lettering 🔒

- 🔒 **God / the white power:** clean, borderless, weightless.
- 🔒 **Satan / the dark power:** heavy, cracked balloons.
- 🔒 **Egris's first spoken word** ("Order," Ch.2 p.7) is lettered rougher and drier than
  everyone else's — the first sound from a man silent since death.
- 🔒 Keep these consistent across chapters.

---

## Never 🔒

- Never a glowing Mark, a sigil, or an aura.
- Never a skull, scythe, hood-and-bones, or any theatrical death iconography for the Reaper.
- Never a crown on Egris in Arc 1.
- Never the crown driven into the stone. **That scene is non-canon** — see
  `NON-CANON-EXCLUSIONS.md`.
- Never colour outside the Reaper's single crimson accent.
- Never left-to-right panel order.
- Never a monstrous or horned Satan.
- Never speed lines or motion blur on Suro's draw, or any motion on the Reaper.
