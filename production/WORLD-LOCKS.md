## The Three Territories 🔒

### HEAVEN — God. Order, law, permanence.
Every soul is assigned a role by judgment. No negotiation. 🔒 **Heaven is not cruel — it is
absolute**, and that is worse and harder to argue with. Souls who don't fit their role stop
questioning; whether that is peace or erasure is 🔒 **left to the reader — do not resolve it.**
Architecture: vast, white, geometric; cathedrals with no ceiling; light with no source;
everything clean and proportional. 🔒 The longer you look, the more unsettling it gets: nothing
is out of place because nothing is *allowed* to be.
**Key location:** the Throne Hall. Egris will eventually stand here (not in Arc 1).

### HELL — Satan. Conflict, desire, freedom without structure.
🔒 **Not a prison.** Souls can leave if strong enough; most don't, because leaving means giving
up position. Loud, violent, alive. 🔒 **Completely without loyalty** — every alliance
temporary, every throne contested. Architecture: volcanic, shifting, fortresses built while
others burn, cities that change layout between pages.
**Key location:** 🔒 the **Pit of First Rebellion** — where Satan first refused God. Old stone,
unchanged. His monument to himself, and the one fixed thing in Hell.

### THE WASTES — no one. Forgotten, neutral, unclaimed.
🔒 Neither God nor Satan holds it **because neither wants it badly enough.** Home to forgotten
souls, dead civilisations, abandoned gods (beings who lost all their believers), and the
Hollow Men. Architecture: 🔒 **ruins of every civilisation layered on top of each other** —
Roman beside Mesopotamian beside pre-colonial; the sediment of forgotten history. Gray sky,
dust, wind that sounds like voices. Ground that crunches "like dry cartilage." Mist that moves
like a living thing.
🔒 **Why it matters:** these souls have nothing — no god, no master, no identity. They also
have no chains. That is the foundation Egris builds on.

## The Border Gates 🔒

A plain of **black stone** where all three territories meet — flat, endless, featureless,
polished "like a blade." Sky neither day nor night; gray, lightless, **no horizon line**.
Every soul arrives here first. Thousands kneel.

- 🔒 **The Gate of Heaven** — white, ornate, permanent. Light bleeding through the seams.
- 🔒 **The Gate of Hell** — black, jagged, **always slightly open**.

🔒 **Egris's claim is the walking away, not a planted object.** He grips God and Satan by the
wrists, shoves both crowns to the stone, and walks out between the closing gates.
**There is no crown driven into the stone.** That image belongs to a superseded draft and is
archived — see `NON-CANON-EXCLUSIONS.md`. Never illustrate it.

Canonical establishing shot: `world/concept-art/the-border-gates.png`.

## The Camp 🔒

Egris's settlement in the ruins of the Wastes. 🔒 **Never called a kingdom, never called an
empire in Arc 1**, and it has no name. Ruined pillars, broken stone, open ground.

- 🔒 **43 souls** by Ch.9, holding through Ch.12 ("more arrive each day").
- Named residents: Arghun, Borchu, Suro, the Woman, the Boy, farmers, broken soldiers.
- 🔒 **Nobody in the camp is under orders.** Egris drills no one and commands no one. Borchu
  drills the defences by his own choice. The camp's defining property, in the Reaper's own
  words (Ch.12): *"A place where the dead are not commanded. Where they are not owned."*
- 🔒 **The third path** — named for the first time in Ch.12: *"Not Heaven's order. Not Hell's
  chaos. Something else. Something that has never existed in the Wastes before."*

## Arc 1 Geography — Locked Sequence 🔒

```
Ch.1–2   The Border Plain (black stone, the two Gates)
Ch.3     Into the Wastes — deep mist, broken towers
Ch.4     A ruined settlement; the Hollow Men
Ch.5–12  The camp in the ruins, and the open Wastes around it
```

🔒 **Egris never enters Heaven or Hell in Arc 1.** Heaven is seen only as Seraphel's *vision*
of a golden city (Ch.5). Hell is seen only as a **fissure** opening to let the infernal host
march out (Ch.6).

## End State of Arc 1 🔒

Heaven has withdrawn without striking a blow. Hell has withdrawn unimpressed but patient. The
echo army has dissolved. The colossus is broken. The Mark is paused at the forearm. 🔒 **The
Former Rival is raising a real army — "not of echoes. Of the living dead who believe in his
vision of order"** — and it is visible on the horizon on the final page. 🔒 Per the Reaper:
Heaven, Hell **and the Wastes** will all come next — *"Not to offer. To end."*

## ⚠ Open — Do Not Fill In

- ⚠ **Where and when Egris died.** The finished Ch.1 shows an empty plain of winter grass, a
  deathbed, and a son speaking too softly to hear. It names **no place and no date**, and
  shows no battlefield. (The archived `scripts/` draft opened on "1227 AD. The Mongolian
  steppe" with a Mongol army and a tent — that is non-canon and was not carried forward.)
  Ch.2's "chaos of the steppes" and Ch.3's Samarkand place his *empire*, not his death scene.
  **Do not restore a date card or a battlefield.**
- ⚠ **The Wastes' relationship to the Reaper.** Her bible says she belongs there more than
  anyone, and that the gray detail-drained ground is closest to her true nature. Whether the
  Wastes are *hers* in any formal sense is unstated.
- ⚠ **Abandoned gods.** Named in the world file and the power system; none appears on the page.
