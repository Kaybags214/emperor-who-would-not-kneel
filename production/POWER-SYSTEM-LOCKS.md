## Core Principle 🔒

🔒 **Power in the afterlife is not magic and not divine birthright. It is authority — the
degree to which other beings recognise and submit to your command.** Power is therefore
**relational**. You cannot be powerful alone.

This is the engine of the whole series. Do not add a magic system, a stat system, tiers,
rankings, or named techniques. There are none.

## The Three Forms 🔒

| | **FAITH** | **FEAR** | **LEGACY** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held by | God | Satan | Egris |
| Source | Worship and belief across history | Terror, domination, threat of worse | People who freely keep choosing him after death |
| Nature | Vast, ancient, structural; self-reinforcing | Immediate, reliable short-term | The only form that can grow after the source is gone |
| Weakness | Needs maintenance; God cannot afford to be seen as fallible | Needs constant reinforcement; the afraid always seek an escape or a stronger protector | **Cannot be forced** |

### The Legacy trap 🔒 — the spine of the series

```
followers obey from fear      → Egris becomes Satan
followers worship him         → Egris becomes God
followers choose him freely   → Egris remains himself
```

🔒 If Egris's people obey him out of fear, **he is converting his own power into Satan's**. If
they worship him, **he is converting it into God's**. Freely chosen loyalty is the hardest
thing to build and the easiest thing to destroy — one broken promise, one cruelty, and the
structure shifts.

🔒 **This is why Egris refuses every army offered to him.** Not squeamishness, not humility.
Accepting command would destroy the only kind of power that is actually his. Never write him
as merely noble here; he is being precise.

**On-page proof of the mechanic:** the colossus (Ch.11) collapses not because it is beaten but
because the will sustaining it — the Rival's certainty — breaks against people standing
**without being commanded**. The philosophy *is* the combat system.

## The Reaper's Mark 🔒

🔒 **It is a mortality countdown on Egris. It is NOT a resource he spends, and it costs his
followers nothing.** (An earlier concept had it cost a follower's memory per use. That is dead
— it appears nowhere in the finished chapters. See `NON-CANON-EXCLUSIONS.md`.)

- 🔒 Draws on the Reaper's authority over death, which **predates all three forms** and fits
  none of them.
- 🔒 It grows **on its own**, one stage at a time, **each time Egris refuses a major offer of
  authority.**
- 🔒 **Left hand/arm.** Consistent across every appearance.
- 🔒 **A black vein under the skin. Never a sigil. Never glowing. Never an aura.** "Just wrong.
  Like something leaking through." Ch.7's own script note: *"Do not glow the Mark."*

🔒 **Ch.12 is a pause, not a cure.** *"You have time. Not much. But some."* /
*"Do not mistake this for mercy... I am giving you enough rope to see whether you hang
yourself or build a ladder."* The debt stands: *"I will take what is owed."*

🔒 **The escalation across chapters is the storytelling.** No single panel carries it. Draw
the stages exactly as tabled above — the reader should be able to measure the cost by looking
at his arm.

⚠ **AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED — where the Mark came from.** Egris already bears it, and already
knows who is behind it, the first time it is shown. Ch.7 p.17: *"She's been following the
mark."* — and the script direction reads *"He has felt this presence before."* Nothing in
Ch.1–6 shows him receiving it and nothing explains it. Either it predates Ch.1, or it was
acquired in an unwritten beat, or Ch.7 is the moment of acquisition and Egris's recognition
needs a different justification. **Not resolved here. Do not invent an origin scene.**

## Secondary Powers 🔒

- **Hollow Men** — feed on forgotten souls; grow stronger as people lose memory and identity.
- **Abandoned gods** — small reserves of faith left over from dead civilisations. Dangerous
  but limited. Referenced; none on the page yet.
- **Former kings and generals in the Wastes** — hold fragments of legacy; they were once
  followed, and some of those followers are still out there.
- **Echo Warriors** — bound by **duty that survived death**, not by faith or fear. They are
  released by being told the truth, not defeated. Thirty of them.
- **The colossus (Ch.11)** — ash, bone and ruin, twenty feet, a void-heart that pulls light
  in, animated by the Former Rival's will. 🔒 It cannot touch anyone but Egris without
  permission to harm them. It collapses when the will behind it breaks.

---

## Hard Prohibitions 🔒

- No power levels, tiers, rankings, or numeric stats.
- No named attacks or techniques.
- No training arcs or power-ups. 🔒 **Egris gets weaker as the arc proceeds, not stronger** —
  the Mark is the cost curve.
- No magic items. The crowns are offers, not artifacts, and both are refused.
- 🔒 Fights are won by argument, will, or refusal. Ch.5, Ch.6, Ch.10 and Ch.11 all resolve with
  **no decisive blow struck by Egris**. Do not add battles to "fix pacing."
