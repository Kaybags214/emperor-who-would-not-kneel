# THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL
## Chapter 9 — "The Gathering Dark" *(REVISED DRAFT — merges original Ch.9 + Ch.10)*

*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.
Black-and-white. Heavy ink shadow, cinematic negative space, screentone ash.
Approx. 28 pages.*

> **Revision draft by Nex for Kay's review.** This chapter now carries the whole Echo Warrior story (old Ch.9 setup + old Ch.10 refusal) so the offer and the answer happen in one chapter instead of being stretched across two. New: the scouts are on the page and one of them is lost; Arghun's book gets a real job; the Centurion (Varro) does not simply fade, he stays. Kept: "They look to you more each day," the thirty fists, "An army without a general is a mob," Borchu's "third time" speech.

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Marrow's loss (Page 7):** the Hollow touch is quiet, not gory. His expression *smooths away* like an erased drawing. This is the first on-page loss in the arc; give it room.
- **Arghun's pages (Pages 10–11, 26):** establish the rule clearly: the Hollow feed on what is forgotten, so written names resist them. Keep it small and practical, not magic. Ink on hide. A scribe's stubbornness.
- **The temptation (Pages 16–17):** this is the first time Egris visibly WANTS the power. The Mark is quiet when he imagines commanding and only burns when he refuses. Plant that detail; it pays off in Ch.11–12.
- **Page 22 splash:** a release, not a death. Soft screentone, sparks rising.
- **Varro** stays as an individual, not a soldier. He stands next to Egris, never behind him.
