# THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL
## Chapter 11 — "What Walks the Wastes" *(REVISED DRAFT)*

*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.
Black-and-white. Heavy ink shadow, cinematic negative space, screentone ash.
Approx. 28 pages.*

> **Revision draft by Nex for Kay's review.** The big change: **refusal does not work on the colossus.** It isn't asking for anything, so there's nothing to refuse. The Rival's single command breaks, the camp's brave line is not enough, and Egris does the one thing he swore not to do: he gives real orders. They save the camp. They cost him Daro, who dies holding a position because Egris told him to. Kept: "Where do you want us?", the ragged line, Suro's cut, Borchu and the Woman's "Because we choose to."

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The colossus design:** 20 feet tall, ash/bone/ruins, broken helm for a head, a void-heart in the chest, arms of fused weapons and stone. With the Rival's nail cracked, it is pure hunger. It isn't evil, it's a drain.
- **Pages 9–12: refusal fails.** This is the key structural fix. The line holds bravely and it does NOT matter. Let the reader feel the theme fail.
- **Page 15 (the command):** the biggest lettering in the book. Egris's balloon changes shape: rough, heavy, hard-edged. Readers should feel how good it is and how frightening.
- **Page 17 inset:** the Mark *recedes* slightly when he commands. Small, clear, unmistakable. Ch.12 pays it off.
- **Daro (Page 22–24):** he dies because he obeyed. Don't over-dramatize. He's just still standing where he was told. Quiet on the page.
- **Page 25:** Egris on his hands and knees digging is the closest he comes to kneeling. Don't label it. Let readers see it.
