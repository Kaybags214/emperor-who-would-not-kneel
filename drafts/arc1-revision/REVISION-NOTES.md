# Arc 1 Revision Notes *(DRAFT by Nex, for Kay's review)*

**Rule followed:** Kay's original files are untouched. Every revised chapter is a separate `-REVISED.md` file in this folder, with a note at the top saying what changed. Kay's voice, script format and best lines were kept on purpose.

**Chapter count:** still **12**. Old Ch.9 and Ch.10 (the Echo Warriors) were **merged** into revised Ch.9, which freed up the Ch.10 slot for a new chapter. That chapter fixes the Rival's motives and gives the camp its own laws, so it does work the arc needed instead of adding padding.

**Approx. page count:** ~320 → ~326 (Ch.9 28, Ch.10 24, Ch.11 28, Ch.12 30).

## The five big changes

1. **Refusal stops working.** In the original, Ch.9–12 repeat one beat: an offer, a refusal, a speech, and the threat dissolves. Now the echoes *mostly* dissolve, but their Centurion (Varro) stays, and the Hollow that trailed them become a new problem (Ch.9). The colossus **isn't offering anything**, so there's nothing to refuse, and the brave line doesn't stop it (Ch.11).
2. **Egris slips, and it costs him.** To save the camp he gives real orders, and they work. Daro, the young soldier who asked "will we have to obey you?", dies holding a position because Egris said "until I say." Borchu's trust cracks, and eleven people, the woman in red and the boy among them, leave for the Rival's walls (Ch.11–12).
3. **Egris is tempted for real.** The Mark (the Reaper's countdown) *eases when he commands* and *surges when he refuses*. Ruling is literally the medicine for his dying. The Reaper names this in Ch.12 and pauses the Mark "so that fear of me does not choose for you."
4. **The Former Rival makes sense start to finish.** His line is now consistent: he wants fewer dead, he believes structure is mercy, and he admits an old grudge. He sends the echoes as an *offer* (soldiers for Egris), plans the colossus as a *demonstration* to frighten the camp into his walls, loses control of it, then **breaks with Satan's envoy** and starts building an army of people who choose walls (Ch.8, 10, 12).
5. **The camp becomes a people.** On-page losses with names (Marrow, Kesh, Daro). Arghun's book of names becomes a real defense: the Hollow avoid written names. The Hanged Woman (now **Hedda**) leads the writing of the **Four Laws**, the seed of Arc 2.
