# THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL
## Chapter 10 — "No Kings in the Ash" *(REVISED DRAFT — new chapter content)*

*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.
Black-and-white. Heavy ink shadow, cinematic negative space, screentone ash.
Approx. 24 pages.*

> **Revision draft by Nex for Kay's review.** The old Ch.10 (Echo refusal) now lives inside revised Ch.9. This slot becomes a breather-and-build chapter: the camp writes its first rules (led by the Hanged Woman, now named **Hedda**), and we finally see the Former Rival's side of the board, which makes his motives one continuous line. The chapter title still fits, better than before: "no kings" is now the camp's first law. It ends on the colossus waking.

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **This is a breather chapter with a spine.** Two "ledgers" mirror each other: Arghun's banner of names (freely given, no ranks) and Corvane's numbered ledger (names plus numbers plus ranks). Draw them so readers notice the rhyme.
- **Page 11 (the first law):** the camp sits in a RING, not facing Egris. He stands at the outer edge. Composition is the theme.
- **Hedda:** broad-shouldered, rope scar, a sailor's forearms. Moves like someone used to heavy work and bad weather. She is not Egris's rival; she's what Egris's refusal makes room for.
- **Velis:** never drawn menacing. Warm, amused, reasonable. The black rings are the only tell.
- **The colossus's birth (Pages 23–24):** it is not built by the Rival. It is *woken*. The Rival only gives it a single command with Velis's nail. This matters in Ch.11.
