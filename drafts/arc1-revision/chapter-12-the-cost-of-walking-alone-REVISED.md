# THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL
## Chapter 12 — "The Cost of Walking Alone" *(REVISED DRAFT)*

*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.
Black-and-white. Heavy ink shadow, cinematic negative space, screentone ash.
Approx. 32 pages (30 + 2-page stinger).*
*ARC 1 FINALE*

> **Revision draft by Nex for Kay's review.** The finale now pays for Ch.11. Borchu's trust cracks. The woman in red leaves with the boy and nine others for the Rival's walls, using Law Four, the law Egris believes in most. Egris writes Daro's name himself. The Rival breaks with Velis. The Reaper's scene keeps all her best lines and adds one new idea: **the Mark eases when Egris commands.** Ruling is the medicine, and that makes it a temptation. She pauses the Mark so fear of death isn't what decides for him. Ends exactly where the original did: the Rival's army on the horizon.

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Pages 2–4 (the brush):** small and quiet. The first thing Egris writes. His hand shakes.
- **Borchu (Pages 5–7):** shame, not rage. He doesn't leave. He just can't look at Egris until Page 29.
- **The departure (Pages 8–11):** no villainy. The woman is reasonable. The boy's backward look is the hook for Arc 2.
- **Pages 13–14:** the Mark surges the moment Egris vows not to command again. That vow is itself a refusal. Show it as violent.
- **The Reaper:** eyes dark and human in this scene (no reveal; save the crimson ring for Arc 2). Her robes don't move. No smile this time; she's grave, not amused.
- **Pages 31–32 (stinger, Kay's twins Icarus and Incubus):** back to back on one pillar. They are never drawn apart beyond the [LINK]'s reach (Kay's one-second rule). Plants the **doubt thread**: their aura can't touch a man who knows himself, and Daro's death and the Mark are what will make Egris doubt. The wrist touch on p.32 is their loyalty under opposite masters.
- **Page 30:** the eleven walking between the two camps should be tiny but readable.
