# Character Update — Varro, the Centurion *(FLESHED OUT — "the ROMAN CENTURION" from Arc 1, Ch.9–10; named and kept alive in revised Ch.9)*

> In the original, the Centurion dissolves with the other Echo Warriors. In the revision he is the one echo who doesn't, and that breaks the "threat dissolves" repeat.

## Role
The last Echo Warrior. A soldier learning, late and clumsily, how to choose. He gives Egris a sounding board who understands command from the *inside*.

## Look (for a future artist)
Translucent like all Echoes: a faint outline of a stocky, square-jawed man in his forties, close-cropped hair, a broken nose, a deep laugh-line on one side. His **armor is solid and real**: a cracked segmented legionary-style breastplate, a helmet with a split crest (usually carried under his arm, not worn), a short sword, and a battered rectangular shield. A small **pale blue fire** burns where his heart should be, much smaller than the other echoes' had. It grows a little each time he makes a choice of his own.

## Voice
Dry, deadpan, soldierly. Calls his own suggestions "suggestions" and is the only person who can make Egris almost laugh.

## Backstory
Eighteen years in the ranks to earn a farm he never saw. Died on a frontier no one remembers. After death his duty kept walking without him; then a commander with a divided cloak found the echoes and pointed them at Egris.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** to find out what he likes. (He suspects it's farming.)
- **Fear:** that without orders there's nothing in him, that he'll fade like the others.

## Arc
- **Revised Ch.9:** "I have obeyed for longer than your empire existed. I would like to see what it is to choose. Badly, probably."
- **Revised Ch.11:** holds the arch beside Daro, and survives because Egris said "OUT" to him and not, in time, to Daro. That survivor's weight is his Arc 2 burden.
- **Arc 2:** teaches the camp to build walls without ranks; is offered his old rank back by the Rival (Ch.6) and declines; tends the first garden in the Wastes (a single ash-gray seedling) as the arc's quiet image of hope.

## Sample Lines
- "This is a suggestion. A very good one."
- "Faster would be — appreciated —"
- "He held."
- "I was very good at obeying. It turns out that's not the same as being good."
