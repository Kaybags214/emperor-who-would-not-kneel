## A. Backstory (file 1, as relayed)
- **Icarus = Ambition / Ego.** Serves **God**. A rigid "cruel angel" of absolute law who **secretly wants to BE God**.
- **Incubus = Desire / Id.** Serves **the Devil**. A compassionate "sympathetic demon" who **understands human weakness**.
- The twins are **fiercely loyal to each other** despite serving opposite masters: a tragic tug-of-war.
- **Shared power:** they see a soul **split in half**. Icarus hears its **highest aspirations**; Incubus hears its **darkest thoughts**. At a choice, they present the two halves to Egris (or anyone), so the *reader* has to weigh them.
- **Egris is the third option:** he rejects both and relies on his own will.

## B. Powers & aesthetics (file 2, as relayed). This settles the physical design.
- **Icarus, LEFT hemisphere (logic, language, order).**
  - *Absolute Logic:* sees structure and flaws, predicts moves.
  - *The Word:* spoken truths manifest. "Stop" freezes the air; "Fall" increases gravity.
  - *Look:* sharp geometry, piercing white light, perfect symmetry, wings of blade-like light beams.
- **Incubus, RIGHT hemisphere (emotion, intuition, creativity).**
  - *Emotional Overdrive:* floods minds with grief, ecstasy or hallucination.
  - *Fluid Form:* ink shadows that make nightmares physical. He's an "artist of terror."
  - *Look:* fluid, asymmetrical ink, shifting colors, wings of smoke or liquid that shift with his mood.
- **Combined attack, the "Corpus Callosum" / The Shattered Mind:** a cold logic box plus an emotional flood. The victim sees the truth of their acts while feeling the guilt.
- **Followers:** Icarus's become logical machines without humanity. Incubus's become free but spiral into madness.
- **Egris is the synthesis.** The twins' mind powers have **zero effect** on him.
- *Open (as of file 2):* does the attack physically alter the body, or stay psychological? (See open questions.)

## C. The aura rule (file 3, as relayed in Nex's reply to Kay)
- The twins' core power works as an **aura**. "**Only if you know yourself, you are not affected.**"
- Hypocrites fall apart near them. Honest people walk through, even honest monsters.
- So the attack is **in the mind**.
- *Open:* what happens when the twins **disagree**?

## D. The one-second rule (file 4, as relayed)
- They are **two halves of one mind**. If too much distance or a barrier separates them, for even **one second**, they go **berserk**.
  - **Incubus alone:** pure emotional madness that destroys friend and foe.
  - **Icarus alone:** an unfeeling machine that erases anything imperfect.
  - Berserk, they lose self-knowledge, so **their own aura turns on them**. They tear themselves apart and **can be killed**.
- So they fight as **one synchronized organism**. Enemies can only beat them with terrain, traps or sacrifice: anything that parts them for a heartbeat.
- **Codependency:** their greatest fear is being left alone without the other half.
- **Kay's suggested scene:** Egris effortlessly holds them a few feet apart and watches the second tick, master of their fate.
- *Open:* is their link **physical touch** (hands, back to back) or a **visible energy tether**?

## How the drafts use this canon
- **Hierarchy:** they're Heaven's and Hell's top field agents. Icarus ranks **above Auditor Halveth** (he sent him); Incubus ranks **above Velis**. Because they can't be apart, Heaven tolerates a demon in its halls and Hell tolerates an angel ("Nobody gets one of us cheap").
- **Every appearance is a pair.** They're never drawn farther apart than the link reaches, except in the Ch.12 confrontation, where that's the point.
- **The split soul** (bright half / dark half beside the person) is their signature panel at every real choice: Egris (Arc 1 Ch.5, Arc 2 Ch.9) and the Rival (Arc 2 Ch.11).
- **The doubt thread** (how I reconcile "zero effect on Egris" with the Mark's cost): in Arc 1 the aura goes straight through him because he knows himself. Daro's death and the Mark (which eases when he commands) crack that. In **Arc 2 Ch.9 it reaches him for the first time** and he staggers, doubting. In Ch.10 his own command eases the Mark, and Icarus notices. After the Rival's sacrifice he writes his own name (Ch.12) and knows himself again. In the **Ch.12 climax nothing they do can touch him**, and he holds them apart.
