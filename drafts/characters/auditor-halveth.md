# Character — Auditor Halveth *(NEW — Arc 2)*

## Role
Heaven's second envoy, sent because Seraphel came back from the Wastes *changed*. Seraphel argued; Halveth assesses. He represents the side of Heaven that doesn't need to be cruel because it is so thorough.

## Look (for a future artist)
Architectural, like God's own visual language: a tall angelic figure whose body looks **carved more than born**, with smooth pale stone-like skin, a bald head and no hair at all. Four folded wings made of overlapping white **pages** instead of feathers; a written line appears on a page whenever he passes judgment. Simple white robes with geometric hems. He carries a long **quill of light** and a slate of white stone. His eyes are pure white. Small white flowers rise where he steps, like Seraphel's, but they are perfectly identical.

## Voice
Flat, courteous, procedural. Reads souls' records aloud like inventory. Never angry. When he is displeased, he goes quieter.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** every soul in its correct place.
- **Fear:** a precedent. If the third path is allowed to exist for forty days, it exists forever.

## Arc (Arc 2)
- **Ch.2:** appears in the sky: "Unregistered polity. Assessment begins."
- **Ch.3:** audits the camp, reading each soul's sins aloud and offering immediate entry to Heaven to anyone who renounces the camp. Some accept. He gives the third path **forty days** before "correction." Seraphel escorts him in silence, demoted.
- **Ch.12:** returns at the end of the forty days with the Host. Heaven formally declares the third path a rebellion: the hook for Arc 3.

## Sample Lines
- "Unregistered polity. Assessment begins."
- "You stole bread at nine, abandoned a wife at thirty, and were brave once at fifty-one. Heaven can use the bravery. Renounce, and enter."
- "Mercy is a filing system. You have simply never been filed."
- To Arghun: "We are both record-keepers. Mine is simply final."
