# Character — Magistrate Corvane, the Ledger *(NEW — introduced in revised Arc 1, Ch.10; major in Arc 2)*

## Role
The Former Rival's lawgiver and quartermaster. He keeps the Bastion's great ledger: every soul gets a name, a number and a rank. He's the dark mirror of Arghun, a record-keeper who uses records to sort people, not to save them. He's also the man the Black Samurai (Adé) has been walking toward.

## Look (for a future artist)
Sixties at death. Tall, thin, stooped from desks, immaculate. Pale, papery skin, close-cropped silver hair, clean-shaven, wire spectacles that are *not* cracked (contrast Arghun's). A long dark magistrate's coat with a high collar and a double row of **brass buttons**, all polished. Ink never stains his fingers; he wears thin gray gloves. Carries a huge iron-cornered ledger chained to his belt and a steel-nibbed pen. No weapon. He has never needed one.

## Voice
Soft, precise and reasonable. Speaks in procedure. Never raises his voice and never swears. Calls people by their numbers without noticing.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** a world that adds up. No waste, no chaos, no surprises.
- **Fear:** an unrecorded soul. Anything that can't be put in a column.

## Backstory
In life he was a magistrate of "the Charter," a trading company that ruled a coast by contract. He signed the order to burn a rebellious inland kingdom "for the preservation of order." That was Adé's homeland. He never saw the fire. He remembers the signature perfectly.

## Arc
- **Arc 1 (Ch.10):** introduced at the Bastion's ledger-table: "Number four hundred and twelve. You will be safe here." He asks the Rival the one honest question: "And if the thing doesn't obey?"
- **Arc 2:** the Bastion runs on his ledger, and the ledger hides **the tithe**: every new moon, the lowest numbers are walked out to the Hollow so the rest stay safe. He believes this is mercy by arithmetic. In the climax he tries to tear out the low-number pages to save the high ones. Adé finally faces him. (Kay to decide whether Adé kills him or chooses not to. See the Arc 2 outline.)

## Sample Lines
- "Name. Former trade. You will be safe here."
- "I don't hate anyone. Hatred is imprecise."
- "Every wall has a cost, Commander. I simply write it down so you don't have to."
- To Adé: "I signed a great many things. You'll have to be more specific."
