# Character Update — Hedda, the Hanged Woman *(FLESHED OUT — "the HANGED WOMAN" from Arc 1, Ch.4; named and given a role in revised Ch.10–12 and Arc 2)*

> In the original Arc 1 she had about five lines in Ch.4, then disappeared. The revision makes her the person Egris's refusal makes room for: the camp's first lawmaker.

## Role
Founder of the camp's council (the "Ring of Stones"), and the voice of *structure chosen from below*. She's the third answer between Egris's refusal and the Rival's walls.

## Look (for a future artist)
Forties at death. Broad-shouldered, heavy-armed, sun-and-salt weathered pale skin, ruddy cheeks, straw-blond hair in a thick sailor's braid, a broken nose. A **rope-line scar** around her neck (bloodless, like all Wastes wounds). A ship's officer's coat, too big, taken from the captain she mutinied against, with the epaulettes torn off. A heavy iron ring of **keys** on her belt (the stores keys she took to feed the crew). She uses a belaying pin as a club.

## Backstory
Bosun on a merchant ship. The captain was starving the crew to sell the stores at port, so she took the keys and fed them. The law called it mutiny, and she was hanged at the yardarm.

## Voice
Loud, plain, practical. Hates speeches, including Egris's. Swears at the weather.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** a camp where nobody has to be strong to be safe.
- **Fear:** becoming the captain. She knows exactly how easy it is.

## Arc
- **Arc 1 (Ch.4):** distrusts Egris on sight. "Who are you?"
- **Revised Ch.10:** stops a fight, confronts Egris ("You won't rule. Fine. Then let *us*."), and leads the writing of the four laws.
- **Revised Ch.11–12:** carries the wounded on Egris's order and defends him to Borchu: "He saved us." She quietly reminds Egris of Law Four when the eleven leave.
- **Arc 2:** runs the council through its first real crisis, when two of its own laws collide (Law Two vs Law Four, Ch.2). In Ch.9 she co-designs "the Ladder": rotating, elected command for one battle at a time.

## Sample Lines
- "Down you go."
- "You know who gets the dry corner when there's no law? Him. Every time."
- "You won't rule. Fine. I don't want you to. Then let *us*."
- "I hate a lord's law more than you ever will."
