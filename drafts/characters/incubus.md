## Kay's canon (copied word for word from the sheet. Do not change it.)
- **Title:** VOICE OF THE BLEEDING DARK
- **Origin:** Unknown
- **Domain:** Desire, Emotion, Chaos, Instinct (Right Hemisphere)
- **Alignment:** Chaos; Nature; Power; Instinct
- **Features / Remarks:**
  - Embodiment of beautiful madness. His presence bends reason, stirs longing, and unravels the weak.
  - Unpredictable and mercurial. His form shifts with emotion and desire.
  - His power overwhelms the rational mind, awakens the forbidden. To gaze too long is to drown.
- **Quote block:** "He does not whisper. He floods. He is the ache behind pleasure, the hunger behind dreams, the darkness that loves you back."
- **Power Study: DISTORTION OF DESIRE.** "Flameform of primal lust. Emotion condensed. Feeds on longing. No form. Only hunger." (Shown as a screaming, fanged face made of dark tendrils.)
- **Look (from the art):** Black man with a huge wild afro. Dark cracked-vein patterns spread over his skin like fractured glass. Small hoop earrings. Black ink drips from his lower lip. Lean, bare-chested under a torn dark wrap, with chains and ornaments on his belt and wrapped legs. His body dissolves at the edges into tattered black flakes and tendrils, like a cloak made of the dark itself. One hand reaches out toward the viewer.

## Kay's backstory canon (from Kay's notes, relayed; full summary in `the-twins-CANON-from-Kay.md`)
- **Desire / Id. RIGHT hemisphere (emotion, intuition, creativity).** Serves **the Devil**. A compassionate "sympathetic demon" who **understands human weakness**.
- **Powers:** *Emotional Overdrive* (floods minds with grief, ecstasy or hallucination). *Fluid Form* (ink shadows that make nightmares physical; "artist of terror"). Shared: sees a soul split in half and **hears its darkest thoughts**. The **aura**: "only if you know yourself, you are not affected."
- **Look:** fluid, asymmetrical ink, shifting colors, wings of smoke or liquid that shift with his mood.
- **Followers** become free but spiral into madness.
- **Twin bond:** fiercely loyal to Icarus. **One-second rule:** apart for one second, he goes berserk into pure emotional madness that destroys friend and foe, until his own aura turns on him. His greatest fear is being left alone.

## How the drafts use him (Nex; Kay to approve)
- **Rank:** Hell's top field agent, above Velis.
- **Voice:** warm, honest, too close. "I won't lie to you. I never do." He apologizes *before* doing his master's work. He says "brother" to Icarus.
- **His Arc 2 motive:** the Forgetting is the opposite of everything he is (no desire, no grief), so he hates it on sight (Ch.7) and helps hold the line (Ch.10). Out of compassion, he pulls his flood back when Egris shows him people drowning.
- **His big beat:** the Rival carries his longing *into* the dark instead of drowning in it: "Nobody's ever taken it *with* them" (Arc 2 Ch.11).
- **Content note:** Kay's sheet says "primal lust." The drafts play his power as longing, grief, ecstasy and terror, with no sexual content on the page, per the seinen/no-explicit-content rule. Flagged for Kay.
- **Color note:** Kay's red variant sheet is gorgeous, but the drafts reserve crimson for the Reaper's blood and eye-rings. Kay's file 2 also says his look has "shifting colors." Suggestion: B&W interiors, with red and shifting color for covers, color pages and merch. Flagged.
