# Character — Tavi, the Silent Boy *(FLESHED OUT — "the BOY" from Arc 1, Ch.4–12; named and given a voice in Arc 2)*

## Role
The silent boy the woman in red protects. In Arc 1 he never speaks. His backward look at Egris on Ch.12, Page 11 is the hook. In Arc 2 he becomes the camp's moral surprise: the one who frees the Bastion without anyone giving an order.

## Look (for a future artist)
About ten. Thin, big-eyed, dark-brown skin, close-cropped hair, an oversized tunic belted with twine, bare feet. He clutches one of Arghun's torn name-pages (his own) folded small in his fist. In the Bastion, a wooden **number tag** hangs around his neck: **No. 2,017**.

## Voice
Nothing in Arc 1. In Arc 2, when he finally speaks, his voice is small, plain and terribly direct. Short sentences. He doesn't know how to be polite to power.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** to keep his name. Nobody can take what you never say out loud. That's why he stopped talking.
- **Fear:** being called by a number.

## Backstory (Arc 2 reveal)
He and his parents died together. The Hollow found his parents first in the Wastes. He watched their names leave their faces and decided the only safe place for his own name was inside his mouth, unspoken.

## Arc
- **Arc 1:** silent witness. Leaves with the woman in red for the Rival's walls (revised Ch.12). Looks back.
- **Arc 2 (Ch.5–7):** his number is called for the tithe. He survives, and in Ch.7 he speaks for the first time, on the Bastion wall, to two thousand numbered souls: "You can leave. It's a law. I saw them write it." The exodus that follows is nobody's command.

## Sample Lines
- (Arc 2, his first word, to Egris) "...Tavi. My name. You didn't ask either."
- "Numbers don't have mothers."
- "You can leave. It's a law. I saw them write it."
