# Character Update — Adé, the Black Samurai *(FLESHED OUT for Arc 2 — supplements `characters/the-black-samurai/the-black-samurai.md`, does not replace it)*

> Kay's original bible stays canonical for his look, code and themes. This update only answers the open question "how does he enter the story?" and ties his revenge spine to the Arc 2 cast. Concept-art note: his existing sheet is labeled 黑風 (Kokufū / "Black Wind"), an earlier working title. Keep "Black Wind" as the epithet people in the Wastes use for him, so the art stays usable.

## Role in Arc 2
A masterless swordsman walking through the Wastes toward **Magistrate Corvane**, the man who signed the order that burned his homeland "for the preservation of order." He enters in Arc 2 Ch.1 passing *through* Egris's camp, refusing hospitality, and ends up defending its children anyway (his bible's story hook #2: "the ones he can't leave behind").

## How he fits the theme
- **Egris** refuses thrones. **Suro** refuses to strike first. **Adé** refuses to kneel and refuses to *forgive*. He's the camp's test of whether the third path has room for justice, or only for mercy.
- **Adé and Suro** are two Black swordsmen with opposite codes. Suro waits; Adé arrives already decided. Their night spar in Ch.2 has no blood, but it's the best dialogue scene in Arc 2.
- **Velis** offers him Corvane gift-wrapped (his bible's hook #3: "the offer"). He refuses because the gift comes with a master.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** Corvane to *remember* the people he signed away, by name, before the end.
- **Fear:** that killing him will feel like nothing, and he'll have walked all this way to become a man who signs orders with a sword.

## Arc 2 Beats
Ch.1 arrives; Ch.2 spar with Suro; Ch.4–5 infiltration, nearly kills Corvane at the tithe and Suro stops him; Ch.10 faces Corvane during the battle as Corvane tears out the low-number pages. **Decision for Kay:** (A) he kills Corvane and lives with it, or (B) he makes Corvane read every name on the torn pages aloud and walks away, letting Corvane's own ledger judge him. Nex recommends B, the stronger seinen ending and the most "would not kneel."

## Voice
Quiet, sparse and formal. He says less than Suro and lands harder. Occasional dry humor with children only.

## Sample Lines
- "I am not passing through your camp. I am passing through the Wastes. Your camp is in the way."
- To Suro: "You wait for the other man to move. I've already moved. Forty years ago."
- To Velis: "A gift with a master inside is just a collar with a bow on it."
- To Corvane: "You'll be specific. You'll read them. All of them."
