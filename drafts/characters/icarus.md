## Kay's backstory canon (from Kay's notes, relayed; full summary in `the-twins-CANON-from-Kay.md`)
- **Ambition / Ego. LEFT hemisphere (logic, language, order).** Serves **God**. A rigid "cruel angel" of absolute law who **secretly wants to BE God**.
- **Powers:** *Absolute Logic* (sees structure and flaws, predicts moves). *The Word* (spoken truths manifest: "Stop" freezes the air; "Fall" increases gravity). Shared: sees a soul split in half and **hears its highest aspirations**. The **aura**: "only if you know yourself, you are not affected."
- **Look:** sharp geometry, piercing white light, perfect symmetry, wings of blade-like light beams.
- **Followers** become logical machines without humanity.
- **Twin bond:** fiercely loyal to Incubus. **One-second rule:** apart for one second, he goes berserk and becomes an unfeeling machine that erases anything imperfect, until his own aura turns on him. His greatest fear is being left alone.
- This fills the **Weapon / Power** blanks on the Drive sheet. (My earlier "Order-Wing Array / Alignment" proposal is withdrawn.)
