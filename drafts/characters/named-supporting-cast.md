# Supporting Cast — Named in the Revision *(NEW NAMES for existing unnamed characters)*

> These characters were already on the page in Arc 1 without names. The revision names them so their losses and choices land. If Kay prefers other names, they're easy to swap.

## Daro — the Young Soldier *(Arc 1, Ch.5–6, 10–11)*
The young soldier who asked, "If you take it... will we have to obey you?" In revised Ch.10 he finally tells Egris his name: "You never asked." He tells Egris he'd follow an order if one were ever given. In Ch.11 he does, and dies holding the arch because Egris said "until I say" and didn't say it in time. His sword stays with Egris into Arc 2.

**Look:** late teens, slight, dark-olive skin, unruly dark curls, mismatched scavenged armor (a leather jerkin with one bronze pauldron), a plain short sword, a round shield with a cracked rim. Open, earnest face.
**Sample lines:** "Mine does. You never asked it." / "If you ever do give an order, I'd follow it." / "Until you say."

## Nahla — the Woman in Red *(Arc 1, Ch.5–12; Arc 2)*
The grieving woman who protects the silent boy. In revised Ch.12 she leads eleven souls to the Rival's walls: "If I'm going to be given orders, I want walls with them." In Arc 2 she's a trusted supervisor in the Bastion, genuinely content, until Tavi's number is called for the tithe (Ch.5). Her choice to walk back out is the emotional core of the exodus (Ch.7). Name revealed in Arc 2, Ch.4.

**Look:** thirties, tall, dark-brown skin, a headwrap and long dress in deep red (rendered as dark screentone), a mourning-bead bracelet, a strong, tired face. In the Bastion she wears a supervisor's gray sash and a low number (No. 88).
**Sample lines:** "Stand? Against that?" / "Because we choose to." / "I'm not angry. But I want walls with them." / (Arc 2) "Numbers don't have mothers. I do."
