# Character — Velis, the Advocate *(NEW — introduced in revised Arc 1, Ch.10)*

## Role
Satan's envoy in the Wastes. She doesn't command and doesn't threaten. She **collects grievances** and offers tools. She carries Satan's presence into Arc 2 without Satan appearing on every page.

## Look (for a future artist)
Ageless, looks about forty. Tall and graceful. Warm olive-brown skin, dark hair pinned up in an elegant knot with one loose strand, a beauty mark, a perpetual small smile that reaches her eyes, which is the unsettling part. A tailored long coat like a well-paid lawyer's, high-necked and cut sharp, over a floor-length dress. On **every finger**, a ring of black metal, echoing Satan's hand in Ch.6. Up close, each ring is worked with a tiny screaming face. She never looks dirty, even in the ash.

## Voice
Warm, amused, reasonable. Asks questions more than she makes statements. Never lies outright; she just leaves the price in small print.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** every soul in the Wastes owing her master a favor.
- **Fear:** nothing, apparently. Her one flinch is when someone refuses her *and* doesn't want anything in return.

## Arc
- **Arc 1 (Ch.10–12):** gives the Rival the iron nail that leashes the colossus. When it fails, she turns his own line on him: "You asked a hole to obey you, commander. Holes fill." He breaks with her. She pockets the nail and stays, smiling.
- **Arc 2:** works all sides: whispers to Corvane, offers Adé his revenge gift-wrapped, and in Ch.8 wakes the buried colossus with no leash at all, because chaos is the only outcome her master always wins.

## Sample Lines
- "Why not both?"
- "Our master was more direct."
- "I don't sell anything, darling. I just notice what you were already going to buy."
- "People who choose walls. How very like him you sound."
