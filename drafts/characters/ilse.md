# Character — Ilse, the Half-Named *(NEW — introduced in revised Arc 1, Ch.9)*

## Role
Camp scout. The first person in the series to survive a Hollow touch. Her half-forgotten state makes her the camp's walking proof of why names matter.

## Look (for a future artist)
Late twenties at death. Wiry, small, quick, built for climbing rigging. Light-brown skin, freckled and weather-darkened; short black hair hacked off with a knife. A burn scar up her right forearm from a galley fire (how she died). A sailor's knotted rope belt with a marlinspike tucked in it. Since Ch.9, a pale gray **handprint scar** on her left forearm where the Hollow grazed her. Barefoot or in wrapped cloth.

## Voice
Fast, wry, salty. Uses jokes the way other people use shields. When the forgetting creeps in she goes very quiet and starts repeating names under her breath.

## Want / Fear
- **Want:** to be useful. To be the one who comes back.
- **Fear:** waking up one day and not knowing she ever knew Marrow.

## Arc
- **Arc 1 (Ch.9–12):** loses Marrow on the page and almost loses herself. Arghun's written page brings her back. She becomes the camp's best scout because the Hollow avoid her. They half-recognize her.
- **Arc 2:** she scouts the Bastion and discovers the tithe (Ch.1), guides the infiltration (Ch.4–5), and in the finale volunteers to carry the combined book of names into the line of fire because "I already know what forgetting feels like."

## Sample Lines
- "Ours. He just agreed loudly."
- "Write him again. Marrow. Bigger."
- "I'm not brave. I'm just harder to eat."
- "You want to know what the Hollow feels like? Like someone tidying your room while you're still in it."
