### PAGE 7
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE SPLASH — inside the Bastion)*

The great yard inside the walls. Two thousand souls, and it *works*. Rows of shelters in neat lines, each marked with number ranges. Fires (no warmth) at exact intervals. A work gang rebuilding a wall, singing a counting song. Children in a circle learning their numbers. Guards in plain gray sashes walking slow rounds. No one fights. No one runs. On the inner walls, banners of plain gray cloth.
NARRATION (box): *No one starved here for purpose. No one wandered. No one was alone.*
NARRATION (box): *He had built cities like this.*

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Page 7 splash must be genuinely appealing.** The Bastion's order is real and good to look at. Readers should understand why Nahla chose it.
- **Egris's lie (Page 6):** "Ochir. Herder." Small lettering. He's never lied in the series before; a reader should notice.
- **Varro's slouch (Page 2):** the only comedy beat; play it straight-faced.
- **Corvane:** never cruel in posture. He reads the tithe like a roster. That's what makes it monstrous.
- **Page 24:** the slate falling is the key image. The boy is completely still.
