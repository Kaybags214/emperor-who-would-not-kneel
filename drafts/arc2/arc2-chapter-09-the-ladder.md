### PAGE 9
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE — the split soul)*

The twins look at Egris together, and he SPLITS in the air before them: two translucent halves of the same man, a bright one on Icarus's side and a dark one on Incubus's. And this time it holds.
ICARUS *(to the bright half)*: "Your highest aspiration: that no one on this rim dies tonight. You could command it. The Mark would thank you."
INCUBUS *(to the dark half, softly)*: "Your darkest thought: you *want* them to beg you. You miss being the one they beg."
Egris, in the middle, staggers. The black veins on his neck pulse in time with the twins' voices. His face shows what we have never seen on it: *doubt.*

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Mark (Page 5):** this refusal pushes it over the shoulder and up the neck to just under the jaw. Its last stop before Ch.11.
- **The Ring on the bridge (Page 12):** four people in a square over the void. No one in the center. This is the Ladder in one image.
- **The vote (Pages 18–22):** two record-keepers counting the same thing together is half the point. The tie is real; Egris's vote isn't a trick, it's a judgment about who can spend lives honestly.
- **Corvane's gloves (Page 27):** the first ink on his fingers. Important, quiet.
- **Adé's name (Page 28):** the "see" step of his arc. "Adébáyò" is from his original bible; spell it with the accents.
- **Pages 7–10 (Kay's twins Icarus and Incubus):** Icarus brings the Throne's offer, Incubus the Devil's; they share the bridge because they can't be apart. Page 9 is their signature power (the split soul, highest aspiration vs darkest thought), and **the first time it reaches Egris**. That's the doubt thread (Kay's rule: the aura only fails on someone who knows himself; Daro's death and the Mark have cracked that). He still refuses. Refusing to be ruled doesn't grow the Mark; refusing to rule (Page 5) does. Icarus's grid makes soldiers straighten *involuntarily*; keep that subtle.
