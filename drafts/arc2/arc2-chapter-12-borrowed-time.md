Heaven. A hall with no ceiling and no end, built of light arranged into geometry: columns that are lines, floors that are grids, walls that are proofs. Everything symmetrical. Everything permanent. ## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The town (Pages 1–5):** the visual opposite of the Bastion. Mixed stone, no grid, clusters. The gate's bar is on the inside (Page 5). Draw it clearly; it comes back on Pages 15 and 20.
- **Law V (Page 3):** "Anyone may climb. Everyone climbs down." The Ladder made law.
- **The seedling (Page 5):** ash-gray, tiny, the first living plant in the Wastes, growing on the scar where the Rival went in. Don't explain it.
- **Corvane (Pages 6–7, 23, 29, 32):** his fate under Kay's Decision 4, option B: alive, reading every night, Adé watching. He's the clearest victim of the Shattered Mind, because he still hasn't fully faced himself. If Kay picks option A, these panels need rewriting.
- **The Mark (Pages 9, 11, 21–24):** it throbs when Egris refuses the honorary seat (refusing to rule). It doesn't move when he refuses Icarus (refusing to be ruled). Arc 2 ends with one thin vein crossing his breastbone toward his heart.
- **The Reaper (Pages 10–14):** eyes dark and human; clean hands. She warns that Heaven is sending "the one who designs."
- **The twins (Pages 15–30, 33, Kay's characters):** never drawn farther apart than the [LINK]'s reach, except on Pages 25–28, where that's the whole point. Incubus serves the Devil and stands at the front of Heaven's Host anyway, because the twins can't be apart for a second.
- **The Word (Pages 21, 24):** Icarus's spoken truths get their own lettering, ruled block capitals in a geometric balloon. "KNEEL" pins the crowd (they're *pinned*, not kneeling) and "FALL" adds gravity. Neither holds Egris.
- **The Shattered Mind (Page 23):** the twins' combined attack, cold logic boxing each mind while the emotional flood fills it. Kay's aura rule: honest, self-knowing souls walk through it (Hedda, Varro, Adé, Tavi, Egris); hypocrites and the self-deceived crumble. Purely psychological in this draft; Kay's still deciding whether it alters bodies.
- **THE KEY CONFRONTATION (Pages 25–28), Kay's suggested scene:** Egris holds the twins apart and watches the second tick. Use thin, identical vertical panels with a decimal caption in each, and degrade the [LINK] and the twins' forms a step at a time. Their fear is the point: their greatest dread is being left alone. Egris's mercy at 0.9 is the thesis: he refuses to be master even of his enemies' fate. **Squares with the doubt thread:** the aura reached him in Ch.9 when he doubted; by now (the Rival's sacrifice, his own name written down) he knows himself again, so nothing they do can touch him.
- **The sky-line (Page 30 on):** the ruled scar stays in every exterior shot for the rest of the series until Arc 3 resolves it.
- **Throne Hall (Page 33):** per Kay's art notes, God is architectural light, not a figure. Icarus's look at the Throne is Kay's canon (he secretly wants to BE God). Keep it to one look. Incubus watching his brother instead is the tragedy seed. The empty square on the grid is the hook.
- **Velis & the nail (Page 31):** the nail survived; Hell isn't done.
