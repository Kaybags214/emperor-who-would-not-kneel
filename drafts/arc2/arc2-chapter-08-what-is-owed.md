## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Forgetting (Page 10):** the colossus from Arc 1 is one finger of it. Smoothed faces pressed into its surface like stones in a riverbed. Huge, slow, not monstrous in the usual way. Empty.
- **The Reaper's reveal (Pages 14–21):** this is her series-defining reveal (her bible's hooks #2 and #3: the eye-ring, and what makes her bleed). The crimson accent appears ONLY on her blood and eye-rings. Everything else stays B&W.
- **Egris's "Take him. Run with the column." (Page 11):** an instruction, not a command; he barely notices. Keep it small. Ch.9 asks the question formally.
- **Borchu (Pages 5–7, 23–24):** his arc payoff. He acts without waiting for anyone's voice. The smile on Page 24 matters.
- **The Rival's rows (Page 25):** visually the opposite of Borchu's runners. Both are rescues.
