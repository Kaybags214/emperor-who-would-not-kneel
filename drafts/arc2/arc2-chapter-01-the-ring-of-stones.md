### PAGE 3
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE SPLASH — the camp, weeks later)*

The camp has changed. A low wall of fitted stone (Varro's work) circles the ruins, with gaps left open on purpose, no gates. The banner of names, longer now, flies from a tall pole. At the center, a RING OF STONES where the laws are carved into a flat slab:
**I. NO ONE KNEELS. II. NO ONE IS LEFT FOR THE HOLLOW. III. NO FISTS FOR ROOFS. IV. ANYONE MAY LEAVE.**
NARRATION (box): *Arc 2 — The Ladder.*
NARRATION (box): *Fifty-eight souls. Four laws. No king.*

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Page 1 deliberately echoes Arc 1, Ch.1, Page 1** (wind made visible). Readers should feel the series restarting with a difference: this time there's a sword in the ground.
- **Page 3 splash:** the four laws carved in stone should be legible. This is the "new status quo" page.
- **The Ring (Pages 4–6):** compose it so Egris is never at the center. Hedda stands; everyone else sits. Egris losing a vote should feel funny and important at the same time.
- **Adé's entrance (Page 11):** match the 黑風 / Black Wind concept sheet. Afro silhouette against pale sky. Lots of negative space.
- **Page 18:** use Adé's bible's visual signature: a single black slash of ink, aftermath before motion. (Suro's signature is the held breath *before*. Keep them visually distinct.)
- **Page 24 flashback:** rougher, shakier linework than the present-day panels. Tavi's tag "2017" must be readable.
