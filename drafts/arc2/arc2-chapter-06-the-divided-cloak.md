## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Flashback pages (6–10):** rough, shaky memory ink, as used for Ilse's memory in Arc 2, Ch.1. Young Egris is not a monster; he's a general doing arithmetic. That is the horror.
- **The burning (Page 10):** Egris didn't set the fire. He *let* it happen to save two days. This pays off the Rival's Arc 1 line, "your restraint... made the cost land on other people."
- **The offer (Pages 14–16):** play it as genuinely good. The reader should want Egris to say yes.
- **The Mark (Page 21):** the Reaper's pause ends here. Past the elbow and into the upper arm. Violent.
- **Tavi (Page 26):** first words in the series. Small, plain lettering. No music, no sparkle.
- **Placeholder:** [RIVAL'S NAME] on Page 9. Replace when Kay decides.
