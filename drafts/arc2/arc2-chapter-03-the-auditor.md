### PAGE 1
**Panels: 2**

**Panel 1** *(wide, full-width top)*
HALVETH descends slowly, wings of pages half-open, rustling. SERAPHEL follows a step behind. Where Halveth's feet touch the ash, small white flowers rise, every one of them **identical**.

**Panel 2**

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Halveth:** clean, smooth, minimal linework, almost architectural, against the gritty hatching of everything else. His wings are *pages*. Make the writing-on-wing gag subtle and consistent: a new line appears every time he judges.
- **The flowers:** Halveth's are identical; Seraphel's are each different. This is the visual argument of the chapter, so let it land on Page 20 without comment.
- **The readings (Pages 4, 8):** each record mixes sin and grace. Heaven isn't lying, which is what makes the offer hard to refuse. Bertil's burned hands should be shown in a small inset.
- **Page 18:** Seraphel learning Daro is dead is the emotional center. Keep it quiet; one crack in a perfect face.
- **Page 25:** a loose line of five with no leader. Same composition logic as the Ring.
- **Page 21 (inserted, Kay's twins Icarus and Incubus):** Icarus is above Halveth: he's who "they sent" Halveth for. Halveth bows but does NOT kneel; kneeling by choice is saved for the Rival in Ch.11. Incubus (the Devil's) sits in Heaven because the twins can't be apart for a second; Heaven tolerates him as the price of the Architect. Draw his ink-smoke staining the white floor.
