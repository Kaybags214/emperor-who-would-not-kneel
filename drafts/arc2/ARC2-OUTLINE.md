# Arc 2 — The Ladder *(DRAFT OUTLINE by Nex, for Kay's review)*

> *"I am giving you enough rope to see whether you hang yourself or build a ladder."* (the Reaper, Arc 1 finale)

## Logline
Refusing thrones is no longer enough. Egris's camp has to *build* something, laws and a way to defend itself, without crowning anyone. Across the Wastes, the Former Rival's walled Bastion offers what frightened souls want most: safety, order and a number. Then the camp discovers what the Bastion's safety costs.

## Theme / Core Question
**Can free people build structure without building a throne?** Arc 1 asked whether a man could refuse every master. Arc 2 asks what that man's people do *next*. The answer isn't Egris's refusal or the Rival's walls. It's a third thing that neither man can make alone: **a ladder**, a structure anyone can climb and anyone can step down from.

## Starting State (end of revised Arc 1)
- Egris's camp: **58 souls**, the Four Laws (No one kneels / No one left for the Hollow / No fists for roofs / Anyone may leave), a council led by Hedda, Arghun's banner of names, which the Hollow avoid.
- **The Mark:** paused at the elbow by the Reaper. It eases when Egris commands and surges when he refuses.
- **The Former Rival:** has broken with Satan's envoy Velis. He's gathering a real army of souls who *choose* walls at the **Bastion**, run by Magistrate Corvane's numbered ledger. Eleven of Egris's people, including the woman in red (Nahla) and the silent boy (Tavi), have just walked there.
- **The colossus:** buried, not dead, under the fallen aqueduct.
- **Heaven:** Seraphel came back changed. Heaven will send someone less persuadable.
- **Daro and Kesh:** dead. Borchu's trust is cracked.

## Chapter Outline (12 chapters)

### Chapter 1 — "The Ring of Stones"
Weeks after the aqueduct. The council works, slowly and loudly, and Egris sits in it as one voice and gets outvoted. The Hollow probe the camp every night and turn back at the banner of names. A stranger walks out of the ash: **Adé, the Black Wind**, who refuses food, a name-page and a roof. He says he's passing through toward "a magistrate who signs with brass." When the Hollow hit the children's shelter that night, he defends it without being asked, then tries to leave before anyone can thank him. **Ilse** returns from scouting the Bastion with what she saw at its edge on the new moon. **Ends on:** "They're feeding them to the Hollow. The lowest numbers. Every new moon. And I saw the boy's tag. Two thousand and seventeen."

### Chapter 2 — "Two Laws"
The council splits. **Law Two** (no one left for the Hollow) says go and get the eleven; **Law Four** (anyone may leave) says they chose to go. Egris wants to go and *must not order it*. He sits on his hands while Hedda runs the vote, which ends in a tie. Meanwhile Adé and Suro, two swordsmen with opposite codes, have a night spar with no blood and the best dialogue of the arc. Adé admits the magistrate is Corvane, the man who signed the burning of his homeland. The tie breaks when **Borchu** votes to go, then says he'll stay to hold the camp himself: "Someone here should be a man who won't wait for your voice." **Ends on:** a white page in the sky, and a voice: "Unregistered polity. Assessment begins."

### Chapter 3 — "The Auditor"
**Auditor Halveth** descends, with Seraphel beside him, demoted and silent. Halveth reads each soul's record aloud and offers immediate entry into Heaven to anyone who renounces the camp. It's a real temptation. A few accept, including the Heavy Man from the roof fight, and Law Four makes Egris let them go. Arghun and Halveth face off as two record-keepers ("Mine is simply final"). Halveth gives the third path **forty days** before "correction." Alone with Egris, Seraphel says: "I told them what I saw. They sent *him.*" **Twins (p.21, cutaway):** in Heaven, Icarus orders Halveth's forty days, with Incubus sitting at his feet. **Ends on:** the volunteer party leaving for the Bastion: Egris, Adé, Suro, Ilse and Varro. Borchu watches them go and nods, the first full nod since the arch.

### Chapter 4 — "The Bastion" *(scripted, 24pp)*
The party enters under false names: Egris's first lie is "Ochir. Herder." They get wooden tags (2040–2044). The order genuinely works, and Egris is moved by it. Nahla (No. 88): "Here, nobody has to be brave." Tavi (2,017) holds Egris's sleeve. **Ends on:** Corvane reading the tithe: 1,998, 2,003, 2,011, "And two thousand and seventeen."

### Chapter 5 — "The Tithe" *(scripted, 26pp)*
Ilse walks among the Hollow saying names. Maia (2,003) and the old man (2,011) are saved; 1,998 is lost, his name never known. The Hollow recoil from Egris's Mark. Adé confronts Corvane ("I signed a great many things"). Suro stops the killing blow without drawing: "Kill him here and you make him holy." Adé: "You'll read them. All of them. Not tonight." **Ends on:** the Rival: "Leave us."

### Chapter 6 — "The Divided Cloak" *(scripted, 26pp)*
On the wall: the tithe was once thirty a week; it's three a month now. The Seren valley flashback. The best offer in the series, *your laws, my walls*, and Varro declines a cohort ("I'm a farmer"). Egris's condition is Law Four for every number. The Rival refuses, Egris says no, and the **Mark restarts** past the elbow. **Ends on:** "...Tavi. My name."

### Chapter 7 — "Tavi" *(scripted, 25pp)*
Tavi's silent flashback. Egris refuses to say "come." On the wall Tavi says, "You can leave. It's a law." Nahla: "Numbers don't have mothers. I do." The Rival says, "Open it." **712 leave** (Corvane counted). **Twins:** at the aqueduct rubble, Incubus begs Velis "Don't" and Icarus says "Let her." **Ends on:** Velis planting the nail: "No leash this time."

### Chapter 8 — "What Is Owed" *(scripted, 26pp)*
The Bastion wall collapses, and the Rival orders 1,300 out in rows of ten. **Borchu** carries the banner north without waiting for a vote. The Forgetting rises (the colossus was one finger of it). The **Reaper bleeds**, with the eye-ring reveal: "This is not an ending. This is a theft." "Because you are mine. Promised." "Make it worth watching." **Ends on:** two hosts across the ravine, and Velis watching.

### Chapter 9 — "The Ladder" *(scripted, 30pp)*
On the bridge the Rival offers to follow Egris. Egris refuses command, and the Mark climbs to his jaw. **Twins:** Icarus brings the Throne's offer, Incubus the Devil's. Their split-soul power **reaches Egris for the first time** (the doubt thread), and he refuses anyway. Hedda and Captain Senna form a Ring on the bridge: **the Ladder**, a commander for one battle who steps down at sunrise, and anyone may leave the line. Hedda and the Rival tie at 1,010 each, and Egris casts the deciding vote for the Rival. Corvane takes off his gloves to merge the ledgers. Adé: "Write mine. Adébáyò." **Ends on:** the Rival on the bridge with Daro's sword.

### Chapter 10 — "Names" *(scripted, 30pp)*
The battle is a line of names. **Twins:** Incubus floods the exhausted east line with longing, which holds, then begins to drown them. Egris commands "Now," the Mark eases, and Icarus notices. "Say the names with what they wanted." Corvane tears out the low-number pages by reflex, and Adé makes him read every torn name from memory (**option B**); the names pull the souls back. Adé walks away: "That's everything." Suro strikes first, for the first time. The heart opens. The Reaper: it closes only on a soul walking in fully remembered. Icarus offers to *assign* one; Egris: "No one gets assigned to that."

### Chapter 11 — "The One Who Kneels" *(scripted, 26pp)*
Egris prepares to go in. The Rival stops him: "This is mine." He **kneels to Arghun** and gives his name, **[RIVAL'S NAME]**. **Twins:** they split his soul (to save them / to make Egris watch); he owns both halves and carries Incubus's flood of the green valley *in* with him. "Nobody's ever taken it with them." He gives Daro's sword back. Inside, the Reaper witnesses him all the way down without bleeding. The heart closes. Icarus: "...By a variable."

### Chapter 12 — "Borrowed Time" *(scripted, 34pp, ARC 2 FINALE)*
One town of 1,486 souls; Law V; walls without ranks; an ash-gray seedling. Corvane reads names every night, with Adé listening. Egris refuses an honorary seat. The Reaper: "The rope is shorter. You built the ladder anyway." **Twins:** a night visit, then day forty. Halveth: "Finding: rebellion." Icarus's **Word** ("KNEEL," "FALL"), and the twins' **Shattered Mind**, which the honest walk through. **Egris holds the twins apart and watches the second tick** (0.2…0.9), then lets go: "No one's fate is mine to hold. Not even yours." Icarus summons him before the Throne. Velis and the surviving nail. Egris writes his own name. **Ends on:** Heaven's Throne Hall, Icarus looking at the Throne like a builder, and an empty square left on the grid for Egris.
