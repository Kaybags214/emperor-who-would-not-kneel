## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Page 3, Panel 3 (Egris sitting on his hands):** play it straight, not comic. A conqueror physically restraining himself.
- **The spar (Pages 12–14):** Suro = the held breath *before* (blade half out, perfect stillness). Adé = the single slash, aftermath first. Keep both signatures distinct. **No blood.** The best fight in the chapter is one where nobody gets hit.
- **Borchu (Pages 18–21):** this is the start of repairing his trust with Egris. He's not forgiving him; he's taking a job only he can do.
- **Page 24 (Halveth):** use the clean, borderless, weightless lettering established for Heaven's voice in Arc 1, Ch.1. Seraphel should look *diminished*: same armor, but head lowered, a step behind.
