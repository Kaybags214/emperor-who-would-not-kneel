## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **Pages 1–4:** Tavi's backstory is silent memory-ink. The mother's mouth shaped around his name is the key image.
- **This chapter has no command in it from Egris.** The exodus is Tavi's, Nahla's, and finally the Rival's "Open it." Egris's whole contribution is *not speaking* (Pages 7–8). Suro naming that is the thesis.
- **Page 15:** a full page for two small lines. Don't decorate it.
- **The tags dropping (Page 17):** sound only, `tk`, building. Not everyone drops theirs. Keep it honest.
- **The Rival (Pages 18–19):** his "Open it" is his redemption beginning. He says it quietly.
- **Corvane counts (Page 22):** of course he does. He's right about the danger, too.
- **Page 24 (inserted, Kay's twins):** the tug-of-war. Incubus, the Devil's and compassionate, objects; Icarus, Heaven's and cold, approves. Both outrank Velis. Neither stops her: it's their masters' game, not theirs. Incubus's motive for Arc 2 is planted here: the Forgetting is the opposite of desire and grief. On Page 25, put both twins small in the background of the splash, together.
