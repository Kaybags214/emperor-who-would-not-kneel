## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **New rule shown, not explained (Pages 11–12):** the Hollow won't touch the Reaper's Mark. Death's claim outranks erasure. Don't over-explain; Ch.8 makes it matter.
- **Ilse (Pages 8–9):** spoken names push the Hollow back, an extension of Arghun's written names. Keep it consistent.
- **Page 15:** Adé's slash stops on Suro, who never drew. Show the aftermath first (Adé's signature), with Suro's stillness inside it (Suro's signature).
- **Page 19:** the lost man's name is unknown. That's the cost. Let the silence sit.
- **Corvane, Page 23:** he writes Egris's real name in the ledger. Small panel, big implication.
