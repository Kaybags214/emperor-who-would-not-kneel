### PAGE 16
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE — the split soul, and the last flood)*

The twins look at the Rival, and he SPLITS before them, bright half and dark half. 
ICARUS *(to the bright half)*: "Your highest aspiration: to be the man who saved them."
INCUBUS *(to the dark half, gently)*: "Your darkest thought: you want *him* to watch you do what he never would."

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Mark (Page 5):** black over the jaw and now across the collarbone, reaching for the heart. This is its high-water mark for Arc 2.
- **The kneel (Page 11):** the only chosen kneel in the series. One knee, glaive flat, head *level* (not bowed). He's kneeling to a scribe, i.e. to being remembered, not to a master. Full page, no dialogue.
- **The name (Page 13):** give it its own large balloon. It's written as [RIVAL'S NAME] until Kay picks it. Every later use (Pages 19, 23) must match.
- **The twins (Pages 15–18, 25, Kay's characters):** their signature split-soul power at a real choice. The reader sees the Rival's highest aspiration *and* his darkest thought, and he owns both. That's why the aura can't stop him (Kay's rule: it fails on someone who knows himself). Incubus's tender flood goes *with* him instead of drowning him; his stunned quiet on Page 18 is the most important beat of his Arc 2 story. Icarus's "...By a variable" is the first crack in absolute order. The hand on his brother's head (Page 25) is their loyalty. Keep them within the [LINK]'s reach in every panel.
- **Inside the heart (Pages 20–23):** invert the palette. The void is white; the Rival is dense black lettering. The Reaper's crimson eye-rings are the only accent. Her hands are clean on Page 23, because this is an ending, not a theft.
- **Daro's sword (Page 18):** returned "at sunrise," near enough. Egris keeps it from here on.
