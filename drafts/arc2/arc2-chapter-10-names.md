### PAGE 7
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE — Emotional Overdrive)*

The east readers, drenched in Incubus's flood, cry out not names but LONGINGS, ragged and enormous: "My daughter's laugh!" "Bread from my mother's oven!" "The sea — I want to see the sea again!" "Home!" The Forgetting RECOILS from them, harder than it ever recoiled from a name. But look closer: their eyes are filling black. Ink drips from their lips. Shapes from their nightmares start to rise out of the ink at their feet. One man claws at the air for something that isn't there, wanting so hard he's coming apart at the edges.
INCUBUS *(fierce, tender)*: "You can't forget what you still *want!*"

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Forgetting's touch:** the ground goes smooth and featureless where it passes, like an erased page. Keep it pale against the black night, the inverse of the usual palette.
- **Corvane's betrayal (Pages 12–14):** play it as reflex, not malice: a lifetime of tithes. The tragedy is that it's exactly what he was always doing.
- **Corvane reads (Pages 16–19):** the payoff of "You'll read them. All of them." from Ch.5. Adé's choice is B (no killing). His last line, "That's everything," is the end of his revenge arc.
- **Suro strikes first (Page 21):** a series first. Give it a full-width panel.
- **New one-off names (Page 16–18):** Odile, Petru, Ivo, Sabine, Mott and Merrin, Old Halla. All invented and safe to change.
- **The Reaper (Pages 25–27):** crimson only at her eye-rings and the blood at her mouth. She won't say "dies." She can't lie, so she refuses to answer instead.
- **Pages 6–8 (Kay's twins):** Incubus's Emotional Overdrive holds the line, because longing is a kind of remembering, then starts to drown the readers; nightmare shapes rise from the ink (his Fluid Form). Icarus stays within the [LINK]'s reach, disapproving: the tug-of-war. Egris's "Now." is a *command*, and the Mark eases a hair (Page 8, Panel 3). The synthesis line, "a name with what they wanted," is the third path. Incubus pulls back out of compassion, not obedience.
- **Page 28 (Kay's twins):** Heaven's answer to the heart is *assignment*; Egris's answer is *choice*. That sets up Ch.11, where the Rival volunteers. Egris quoting Hedda's "Everything good is" (Ch.9) is deliberate. The Reaper ignoring both twins is deliberate too: they don't outrank Death.
