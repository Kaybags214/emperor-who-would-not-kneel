# Merch Ideas *(for FUTURE print-on-demand only; nothing set up, nothing for sale)*

> Print-on-demand means nothing is printed until someone orders, so there's no inventory. **Rights first:** the current concept art looks AI-generated. Before selling anything with character art, have an artist create or redraw the final designs (with a written agreement that Kay owns the rights), or at least have the art and its source tool's terms reviewed. AI-only images may not be protected by copyright, which means others could copy the designs, and some print-on-demand sites and contests restrict AI art. **Text-only designs are the safest place to start.**

## Text / quote designs (lowest risk — can start first)
1. **"I WILL NOT BE CONQUERED BY DEAD GODS."** Bold distressed type, black tee, a small crown-on-stone icon.
2. **"WOULD NOT KNEEL."** Minimal back print; series title small on the front chest.
3. **"FEAR THAT KEEPS ITS PROMISES IS STILL FEAR." — Suro.** Thin serif, cream on charcoal.
4. **"REFUSAL MAKES A HOLE. AND HOLES FILL."** Two-line poster with a crack running between the lines.
5. **"ENOUGH ROPE TO BUILD A LADDER."** Reaper quote, with a small rope-into-ladder icon. Good for hoodies.
6. **THE FOUR LAWS.** "I. No one kneels. II. No one is left for the Hollow. III. No fists for roofs. IV. Anyone may leave." Carved-stone look, poster or tee back print.
7. **"NOBODY CHOOSES. MOVE."** For fans after the Ch.11 twist (spoiler merch; release later).

## Character designs (need final artist-approved art first)
8. **Suro, the Blind Blade:** the held-breath stance, blade half drawn, big negative space. The likely best-seller.
9. **The Reaper:** B&W ink with the single crimson accent (blood drop or eye-ring). Poster and art print.
10. **Adé, the Black Wind:** Afro silhouette and a single ink slash. Sticker and tee.
11. **Egris between the Gates:** small figure, two colossal gates. A wide poster; doubles as the series key art.
12. **The Banner of Names:** a long strip of hide pages with names. Fans could order one with their own name added (fun, and on theme).
