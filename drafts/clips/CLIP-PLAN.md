### 1. "The Crowd"
- **Hook (0–3s):** black screen, wind. Text: *"He died. Then he opened his eyes."*
- **Beat:** silent grass pages, an extreme close-up of the eye opening, his cheek on black stone, then the **page-turn reveal** of thousands of kneeling dead. Cut the music at the reveal.
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"Most of them knelt."* Hold on Egris, the only vertical line.
- **Pages:** Ch.1, pp.1–5.

### 2. "Two Gates"
- **Hook:** Old man whispering *"Heaven." ... "Hell."*
- **Beat:** the white figure says "He is mine." The dark figure laughs, "No. He is mine." Both step forward.
- **Cliffhanger line:** narration: *"They had already decided he belonged to one of them. He had decided nothing of the kind."*
- **Pages:** Ch.1, pp.6–12.

### 3. "The Grip"
- **Hook:** *"Nobody has ever touched God."*
- **Beat:** Egris grips both divine wrists; the sky fractures (the p.9 splash). "You offer me the choice between being a golden slave or a black hound."
- **Cliffhanger line:** crowns clatter on stone (SFX only), then black.
- **Pages:** Ch.2, pp.9–10.

### 4. "Dead Gods" *(the trailer clip; pin this one)*
- **Hook:** the crowns on the stone.
- **Beat:** Egris steps over them. *"I have conquered living men."*
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"I will not be conquered by dead gods."* Hard cut to title card.
- **Pages:** Ch.2, p.11.

### 5. "Kneeling or Standing"
- **Hook:** the Hanged Woman: *"You saved us." / "Not if you fear the man who stands in front of you more than the thing behind him."*
- **Beat:** the Hollow Men retreating from will, not violence.
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"We are already dead. The question is whether we die kneeling or standing."*
- **Pages:** Ch.4, pp.27–31.

### 6. "Spared Choice"
- **Hook:** *"Heaven showed him paradise."*
- **Beat:** the golden-city vision splash (Ch.5, p.22). Slow pan. "No one starves. No one fears."
- **Cliffhanger line:** Seraphel: *"They are spared confusion."* Egris: *"They are spared choice."*
- **Pages:** Ch.5, pp.22–25.

### 7. "Will We Have to Obey You?"
- **Hook:** Satan's too-many-fingered hand holding an iron crown.
- **Beat:** the young soldier (Daro, in the revision): *"If you take it... will we have to obey you?"* Egris has no answer ready, then: *"No."*
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"Then why should we stay?" / "Because you choose to."*
- **Pages:** Ch.6, pp.43–47.

### 8. "Holes Fill"
- **Hook:** footsteps, *tk... tk...*, a glaive planted in ash.
- **Beat:** the Former Rival: *"Refusal makes a hole. And holes fill."* Then Suro: *"Fear that keeps its promises is still fear."*
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"You always thought your restraint made you clean. It didn't. It just made the cost land on other people."* Hold on Egris with no answer.
- **Pages:** Ch.7, pp.8–13.

### 9. "She Is Not in the Distance"
- **Hook:** an ash storm, and a figure that doesn't move.
- **Beat:** the storm clears. Empty horizon. *Page turn:* **she is right there.** One drop of near-black blood. *tp.*
- **Cliffhanger line:** *"Every road you choose that is not theirs — I will be at the end of it, waiting to see what you were for."*
- **Pages:** Ch.7, pp.19–22. (This is the strongest scare clip.)

### 10. "The Command" *(revised Arc 1, spoiler-heavy; post late)*
- **Hook:** *"He swore he'd never give an order again."*
- **Beat:** refusal fails; the colossus isn't asking. Egris's voice changes: *"NOBODY CHOOSES. MOVE."* The arch falls.
- **Cliffhanger line:** Varro: *"He held."* Egris: *"I told him to."*
- **Pages:** Ch.11 (revised), pp.12–17, 22–26.
