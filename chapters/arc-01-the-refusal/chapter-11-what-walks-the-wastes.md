*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.

Approx. 28 pages.*

Its arms are pillars of calcified stone and rusted weapons fused together. Where its heart should be: a VOID, a hole that pulls light into it.

It walks slowly. Deliberately. Each step SHAKES the ground.

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The colossus design:** 20 feet tall, made of ash/bone/ruins, broken helm for a head, void-heart in the chest, arms of fused weapons and stone. It should feel like the Wastes given terrible purpose — not demonic, not holy, just WRONG.
- **Page 13 splash (the line forms)** — show the visual contrast between this ragged line and the perfect Echo warrior formation from Ch.9. These people are NOT soldiers. They are individuals choosing to stand together.
- **Page 23 (Suro's cut)** — the ONLY action beat in the chapter. Make it fast, clean, decisive. Suro is not fighting the colossus — he is making a statement.
- **Page 27 (the colossus collapses)** — it does NOT die in battle. It falls apart because the Rival's will sustaining it has been broken by witnessing something he cannot understand. Show it crumbling, not exploding.
- **The Former Rival (final panel)** — just a silhouette on the horizon. He is still alive, still out there, but he has learned something that troubles him.
- **Tone:** This is the action climax, but it is WON through philosophy, not violence. The souls choose to stand, and that choice is the weapon.
