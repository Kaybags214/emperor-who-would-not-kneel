# THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL
## Chapter 3 — "The First Subject"

*Format: Traditional right-to-left manga script. Read panels right-to-left, top-to-bottom.
Black-and-white. Heavy ink shadow, cinematic negative space, screentone ash.
Approx. 18 pages.*

---

### PAGE 1
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1** *(wide, full-width top)*
The Wastes. A jagged landscape of broken stone and swirling mist where geometry has no meaning. The two gates are now thin silver needles on the far horizon — barely visible. EGRIS walks alone across cracked ground, boots crunching on calcified ruins.
SFX: `krch... krch...`
*No dialogue.*

**Panel 2** *(narrow horizontal, center — detail of the ground)*
Close on Egris's boot crushing something white and brittle — not quite bone, not quite stone. Fragments scatter.
SFX: `krnch`

**Panel 3** *(wide, bottom)*
Pull back. EGRIS, a lone figure walking into pale mist and broken towers that lean at impossible angles. The wind is visible — heavy, pushing him rather than blowing past.
NARRATION (box): *The wind in the Wastes did not blow. It pushed. A heavy, stagnant pressure that tasted of cold ash and forgotten names.*

---

### PAGE 2
**Panels: 4**

**Panel 1** *(wide)*
A VOICE from off-panel, thin and fragile like tearing parchment.
VOICE *(off-panel)*: "You are going the wrong way, Great Egris."

**Panel 2**
EGRIS stops mid-stride. His hand drifts instinctively to his empty hip — the place where a weapon should be. He turns his head slightly, not all the way.
*No dialogue.*

**Panel 3** *(wide, the reveal)*
A MAN follows exactly ten paces behind. He is thin, stooped, wearing tattered silk robes of a court official. His fingers are stained permanently black with ink. A heavy wooden satchel hangs across his chest, rattling as he walks.
SFX (from the satchel): `chnk chnk`

**Panel 4**
Close on the man's face — cracked spectacles, wide nervous eyes, sweat despite the cold.
*No dialogue.*

---

### PAGE 3
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1** *(large)*
EGRIS, not fully turning, speaks flatly.
EGRIS: "There is no 'way' here. Therefore, there is no wrong one."

**Panel 2**
The SCRIBE struggles to keep pace, clutching his satchel to his chest.
SCRIBE: "There is the way of the many, and the way of the fool."
SCRIBE: "The many stay near the gates, hoping for a stray crumb of grace or a familiar face. The fools walk into the Wastes to be eaten by the Hollow."

**Panel 3** *(tight)*
SCRIBE: "You appear to be the latter."

---

### PAGE 4
**Panels: 4**

**Panel 1** *(large)*
EGRIS stops completely and turns to face the scribe. The man FLINCHES — a full-body recoil — but he does not run.
*No dialogue.*

**Panel 2**
Close on EGRIS's eyes — calm, reading him.
EGRIS: "You knew me."

**Panel 3** *(large)*
The SCRIBE looks up through cracked spectacles, trembling but holding his ground.
SCRIBE: "I spent twenty-two years in a stone room in Samarkand recording your tax decrees and your execution lists."

**Panel 4**
SCRIBE *(bitter edge creeping in)*: "I know the shape of your signature better than I know the face of my own mother."

---

### PAGE 5
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1**
The SCRIBE steadies himself — a man who has decided to finish what he started.
SCRIBE: "My name was Arghun. I was a scribe of the empire you burned."

**Panel 2**
EGRIS, still unreadable.
EGRIS: "And now?"

**Panel 3** *(wide, ARGHUN clutching his satchel tighter)*
ARGHUN: "Now I am a dead man in a place where even stories die."

---

### PAGE 6
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1** *(large)*
ARGHUN gestures outward at the broken landscape.
ARGHUN: "The Wastes eat memory, Great Egris. Every hour you spend out here, a piece of who you were flakes away."

**Panel 2** *(tight, desperate)*
ARGHUN: "Your first victory. The name of your first horse. The color of your wife's eyes..."
ARGHUN: "The Gray takes it all until you are just another shadow screaming at the wind."

**Panel 3**
EGRIS, quiet.
EGRIS: "If you know this, why are you following me into the dark?"

---

### PAGE 7
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1** *(large)*
ARGHUN looks out at the jagged horizon — broken towers, mist, emptiness. Then back at Egris.
ARGHUN: "Because I have spent my life writing about kings who were afraid of their own shadows."

**Panel 2**
ARGHUN *(softer, exhausted)*: "You just walked away from God and the Devil without looking back."

**Panel 3** *(tight on Arghun's ink-stained hands)*
ARGHUN: "If anyone can hold onto their name in this place, it is the man who made the world fear it."

---

### PAGE 8
**Panels: 2**

**Panel 1** *(large, top — the gesture)*
ARGHUN sinks to his knees — not in worship, but out of sheer exhaustion. His satchel thuds onto the ground beside him.
SFX: `thud`
ARGHUN: "If you are building a kingdom of nothing, you will need someone to write the laws."

**Panel 2** *(wide, bottom)*
ARGHUN, kneeling, looking up at the standing Egris.
ARGHUN: "Even a ghost needs to know what is forbidden."

---

### PAGE 9
**Panels: 3**

**Panel 1** *(large)*
EGRIS reaches down and grips ARGHUN's shoulder — firm, economical. He hauls the scribe to his feet in one motion.
SFX: `grip`

**Panel 2**
The two men standing now, face-to-face. Egris still gripping his shoulder.
EGRIS: "I am not building a kingdom of laws."

**Panel 3** *(wide, both turning toward the mist ahead)*
EGRIS: "I am building a nation of the chosen."

---

### PAGE 10
**Panels: 4**

**Panel 1**
EGRIS releases Arghun and begins walking again. Arghun adjusts his satchel, steadying himself.
EGRIS *(over shoulder)*: "Walk, Arghun. Your ink is dry, but your mind is still sharp."

**Panel 2**
The two men walking side-by-side now — no longer pursuer and pursued. The mist curls around their legs.
EGRIS *(continuing)*: "We will see what the Gray can take from us."

**Panel 3** *(tight on Arghun's worried face)*
ARGHUN *(whisper)*: "And if it takes everything?"

**Panel 4** *(narrow, silent beat — Egris does not look back)*
*No dialogue.*

---

### PAGE 11
**Panels: 1** *(FULL-PAGE SPLASH — closing image)*

Wide shot from behind: EGRIS and ARGHUN walking together into the deep mist ahead. The broken towers loom in shadow. The pale empty sky presses down. Their footprints trail behind them in the ash — the only proof anyone has walked this way.

At the bottom corner, EGRIS's final line:
EGRIS: "Then we will find something new to be."

**— END OF CHAPTER 3 —**

## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **This is a two-person chamber piece** — the whole chapter is Egris and Arghun meeting and walking together. No action, no spectacle. Let silence and posture carry the weight.
- **Arghun's design:** Thin, stooped, cracked spectacles, ink-stained fingers (always visible when his hands are on-panel), tattered court silk, heavy wooden satchel. He is nervous but not cowardly — show it in his body language (flinches but holds ground, trembles but finishes sentences).
- **The Wastes landscape:** jagged, non-Euclidean ruins, mist that moves like a living thing, ground that crunches like dry cartilage. Use heavy screentone for the mist; keep the horizon vague and oppressive.
- **Page 8 (the kneel)** — Arghun kneels from exhaustion, NOT submission. Draw it visibly different from the fearful kneeling at the gates in Ch.1.
- **Final splash (Page 11):** small figures, huge environment. The footprints behind them are important — they are the first mark anyone has made in the Wastes by choice.
- **Tone shift:** This chapter ends on something close to hope. Subtle, fragile, but real. Egris has his first companion.
