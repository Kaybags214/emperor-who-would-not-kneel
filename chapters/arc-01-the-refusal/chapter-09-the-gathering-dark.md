## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The blue-light warriors** are ECHOES — not alive, not Hollow, not demonic. They are warriors whose sense of duty survived death. Draw them as FAINT — visible but translucent, with the blue light burning in their chests as the only solid thing about them.
- **Page 11 splash (the lights stop)** — keep it minimal. Just the lights in the mist, perfectly still. The stillness is the threat.
- **Page 19 (the warriors revealed)** — each echo should be from a different era/culture. Show visual variety: Roman centurion, medieval knight, samurai, Mongol, etc. They are united by PURPOSE, not origin.
- **The salute (Page 21)** — fist over chest, synchronized. This is NOT submission — it is a warrior's acknowledgment of a leader. The threat is: join us or face us.
- **Tone:** Building dread, slow encirclement, the camp realizing they are being hunted by something organized. This is NOT random — someone sent them. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger choice.
