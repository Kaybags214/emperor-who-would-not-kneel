## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **The Reaper's second appearance** — she is NOT threatening this time. She is CURIOUS. Her smile (Page 17) is genuine — she is entertained by Egris, fascinated by what he's attempting.
- **The Mark (Page 18)** — when she touches it, show it FLARE violently, then STOP spreading. It's still there, still dangerous, but frozen at the forearm. This is her gift: time.
- **Page 26 (final splash)** — this must show SCALE and THREAT. The Former Rival's army should be visible as a distant gathering force. Not detailed, just enough to show: something is coming.
- **Tone:** Bittersweet victory. Arc 1 ends with Egris having refused everything and stood his ground — but the cost is becoming clear. The Mark. The gathering army. The Reaper watching. He's won the battle but the war is just beginning.
- **Final narration** — this is the Arc thesis statement AND the Arc 2 setup. It should feel like both an ending and a beginning.
