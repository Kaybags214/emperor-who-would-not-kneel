## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **This chapter is the philosophical core of the arc.** Egris defeats the Echo warriors not through violence but by freeing them from their compulsion to serve. Show this visually — the warriors don't collapse, they RELEASE. Peaceful dissolution.
- **Page 13 splash (the warriors fade)** — this must feel like a release, not a death. Use soft screentone transitions, dissolving linework. The Centurion's eyes on Egris to the last moment.
- **The Reaper's Mark (Page 24)** — show it has grown significantly. It is past his wrist now, creeping up his forearm. Visual reminder: every refusal has a cost.
- **Page 25-26 (the new threat)** — keep it VAGUE. Just a shadow, a shape, movement in the dark. Do NOT reveal it fully. Save that for Ch.11.
- **Tone:** This chapter is a victory, but a hollow one. Egris won without fighting, but he knows something worse is coming. The camp is safer but more vulnerable. The refusals are adding up.
