## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **This is a chamber/dialogue chapter** — three men, one conversation, escalating ideological tension. Let silence and posture carry weight.
- **The Reaper's Mark (Page 1)** — show it has grown. It is a visual reminder that consequences are accumulating.
- **The Former Rival's departure (Page 18)** — this is NOT the last we see of him. Frame it as a deliberate, final break. He is leaving because Egris disappointed him, not because he is afraid.
- **Suro's role:** he is the third voice in the debate, cutting both ways. He is not Egris's ally OR the Rival's — he is observing, judging, testing.
- **Egris's admission (Page 14: "I do not know")** is the emotional core of the chapter. It is honest, vulnerable, and terrifying for a former conqueror to say aloud.
- **Tone:** Reflective, tense, escalating. The chapter ends quieter than it began, but heavier. Egris has refused another path, but the cost is becoming visible.
