## SCRIPT NOTES (for artist / letterer)

- **Reading direction:** Right-to-left. Panel 1 sits at top-right of every page.
- **This is a spectacle chapter.** The Heavenly Host must feel MASSIVE — use scale, light, and overwhelming numbers. They are not evil, but they are absolute.
- **Page 9 splash (sky opens)** — simple, clean, one line of white fire. No detail yet. Let the reveal build.
- **Page 22 splash (the vision)** — show the "perfect" city in full detail so we understand what Egris is refusing. Make it beautiful. Make it soulless.
- **Page 34 splash (the Host descends)** is the centerpiece spectacle. HUNDREDS of angels. This should feel unstoppable.
- **Page 40 splash (the stand)** is the emotional climax. The souls standing is more important than the angels descending. Frame it as a small, fragile, CHOSEN defiance.
- **Seraphel's design:** bright armor, white feather cloak, burning sword. He is not cruel — he is CERTAIN. His face should be almost human but too perfect, too still.
- **Tone shift:** This chapter moves from philosophical debate → vision → ultimatum → defiance. Each beat should be clearly marked visually.
