# Script — Chapter 1: The Last Breath

**Format:** Manga script (panel-by-panel)  
**Pages:** 24 (estimated)  
**Tone:** Quiet, heavy, building tension. No action yet. This chapter is all atmosphere and character establishment.

---

## Page 1

**Panel 1 — Full page splash**

Black. Total darkness. Silence.

At the center, a single line of text:

> *1227 AD. The Mongolian steppe.*

Small in the black.

---

## Page 2

**Panel 1 — Wide**

A battlefield at dusk. Thousands of soldiers. The Mongol army — vast, still, watching. Banners barely moving in wind.

**Panel 2 — Medium**

A tent. Simple. The greatest empire in the world does not need a palace.

**Panel 3 — Close**

A hand. Old. Scarred. Resting still on a blanket. No jewelry. No crown.

**Panel 4 — Wide**

The hand goes still.

**No dialogue. No caption.**

---

## Page 3

**Panel 1 — Full page**

White. Total white.

Then — slowly — the white resolves into something else.

A plain of black stone. Flat. Endless. Cold.

The sky above it is neither day nor night. Gray, lightless, absolute.

---

## Page 4

**Panel 1 — Medium**

Egris from behind. Standing. Already on his feet — he woke standing, not lying down.

He is wearing what he died in. Simple. Worn. He looks like what he is: a man who spent sixty years in the field.

**Panel 2 — Wide**

He sees the plain for the first time. Thousands of souls on their knees, stretching to the horizon. Men and women from every era — Roman armor next to Ming dynasty robes next to pre-colonial African warriors. All of them bowing.

**Panel 3 — Close on Egris's face**

He is not afraid. He is reading the room the way a general reads terrain.

**No dialogue.**

---

## Page 5

**Panel 1 — Wide, low angle**

Two gates. Enormous. They dwarf everything around them.

The left gate: white stone, ornate carvings, warm light bleeding through the cracks.

The right gate: black iron, jagged edges, always slightly open. Heat from inside.

**Panel 2 — Medium**

Egris looks from one gate to the other. He does not move toward either.

**Panel 3 — Close**

He looks down at his hands. Alive in every detail. The same hands. The same scars.

**Caption (internal):**
> *So. Not nothingness.*

---

## Page 6–7 — Double page spread

**Full spread**

The two gates begin to open. From the left: light, structured, organized. From the right: darkness with heat, movement, chaos.

Both opening simultaneously. Both reaching for the same point in the middle of the plain.

Where Egris stands.

**No dialogue. Let the image speak.**

---

## Page 8

**Panel 1 — Close on the white gate**

A figure emerges from the light. Not a man. Something constructed of order itself — light arranged into the suggestion of a form. Vast. Calm. Certain.

**Caption:** *God.*

**Panel 2 — Close on the black gate**

A figure steps through the heat. This one looks more human — almost too human. Sharp features. A small, genuine smile. He looks like someone who has been waiting for exactly this.

**Caption:** *Satan.*

**Panel 3 — Medium, between them**

Egris. Still. Between both figures. Neither looking up at them nor looking away.

---

## Page 9

**Panel 1 — Wide, God speaking**

**GOD:**
> "Genghis Egris. You have arrived."

**Panel 2 — Medium, Satan speaking (overlapping God's energy)**

**SATAN:**
> "He always arrives. That was never in question."

**Panel 3 — Close on Egris**

He says nothing. Still reading.

---

## Page 10

**Panel 1 — God, wide**

**GOD:**
> "You built the largest land empire in human history. You united peoples who had never known peace. You understood what others would not — that divided nations become prey."
>
> "That understanding belongs in Heaven."

**Panel 2 — Satan, moving closer, more casual**

**SATAN:**
> "He understands something else too. That authority comes from strength. That peace imposed from above is just a prison with better architecture."
>
> "That understanding belongs with me."

**Panel 3 — Egris, unmoved**

Still nothing.

---

## Page 11

**Panel 1 — Wide, God and Satan on opposite sides of Egris**

**GOD:**
> "Choose, Genghis Egris. Every soul must pass through one of these gates."

**SATAN:**
> "Choose. Or we choose for you."

**Panel 2 — Close on Egris's eyes**

Something shifts. Not anger. Recognition.

He has been in this exact position before. Two powers demanding he pick a side.

He knows exactly what to do.

---

## Page 12

**Panel 1 — Medium, Egris speaks for the first time**

**KHAN:**
> "You speak to me as though I am a soul to be sorted."

**Panel 2 — Wide**

**KHAN:**
> "I spent my life building an empire. I do not recall asking permission."

**Panel 3 — Close on God and Satan — both still, watching**

---

## Page 13

**Panel 1 — Full page**

Egris turns his back on both gates.

He walks — not toward the Wastes yet, just away from both gates simultaneously. A deliberate act. Every kneeling soul on the plain can see it.

**No dialogue.**

---

## Page 14

**Panel 1 — Wide, God and Satan watching**

**SATAN:**
*(quietly, almost to himself)*
> "Well."

**Panel 2 — Close on God**

**GOD:**
> "He will come to a gate eventually. They all do."

**Panel 3 — Wide, Egris walking**

**Caption (Egris, internal):**
> *They believe that because every soul before me has believed it too.*

---

## Page 15

**Panel 1 — Medium**

Egris stops walking. He looks at the ground in front of him — the exact point between the two gates' reach.

**Panel 2 — Wide**

He crouches down. He is picking something up from the ground.

The kneeling souls near him watch but do not move.

**Panel 3 — Close**

What he picked up: a crown. Simple. Left in the dirt. It doesn't matter which god left it there. It represents both offers.

---

## Page 16

**Panel 1 — Wide, Egris standing with the crown in his hand**

He examines it.

**Panel 2 — Close on the crown in his hand**

**Panel 3 — Egris, full body**

He drives the crown into the stone ground at his feet. Not discarding it — planting it. Like a stake. Like a claim.

**KHAN:**
> "I have conquered living men. I will not be conquered by dead gods."

---

## Page 17

**Panel 1 — Full page**

The crown, driven into the black stone. God on one side. Satan on the other. Egris standing over it.

The kneeling souls in every direction watching.

Silence.

---

## Page 18

**Panel 1 — Close on God**

**GOD:**
> "You have nowhere to go. There is nothing between the gates."

**Panel 2 — Close on Satan**

**SATAN:**
> "You would not be the first soul to refuse. You would be the first to survive it."

**Panel 3 — Egris, turning toward the horizon**

He can see the edge of the plain. Where the black stone ends. Where the Wastes begin.

**KHAN:**
> "Then I will be the first."

---

## Page 19

**Panel 1 — Wide**

Egris begins walking toward the horizon. The edge of the plain. The Wastes.

**Panel 2 — Behind him: God and Satan, both still**

Neither follows. Not yet.

**Panel 3 — Close on the crown, still planted in the stone**

---

## Page 20

**Panel 1 — The kneeling souls**

One of them — a soldier, ancient armor, unrecognizable culture — watches Egris walk away.

**Panel 2 — The soldier looks at the gate in front of them**

**Panel 3 — The soldier looks back at Egris**

---

## Page 21

**Panel 1 — Medium**

The soldier stands up. The first soul on the entire plain to stop kneeling.

The souls around them notice.

**Panel 2 — Wide**

The soldier walks after Egris. One person. Not running. Just walking.

**Panel 3 — Close on God and Satan watching**

**SATAN:**
*(quiet)*
> "That is a problem."

---

## Page 22–23 — Double page spread

**Full spread**

Egris walking toward the edge of the plain. The Wastes visible beyond — ruins, gray sky, the suggestion of a vast and ancient emptiness.

Behind him, at a distance: one soul following. Just one.

The two gates behind them. The crown in the stone. God and Satan watching.

**No dialogue. The image is the chapter.**

---

## Page 24 — Final page

**Panel 1 — Close on Egris's face, looking forward**

Not triumphant. Not afraid. Working.

**Caption (internal):**
> *I have conquered living men.*
>
> *Let us see what the dead are made of.*

**Panel 2 — Small, bottom corner**

The edge of the plain. One step from the Wastes.

**Panel 3 — Black. Title card.**

> **THE EMPEROR WHO WOULD NOT KNEEL**
> Chapter 1: The Last Breath
> *End*

---

## Director's Notes

- This chapter has almost no action. It is entirely about presence and refusal. The art must carry the weight that the dialogue does not.
- Egris should never look small on the page — even when surrounded by divine beings, frame him as someone who fills his space.
- The kneeling souls in the background are important. They are the contrast. Every page they appear on, they are on their knees. Egris never is.
- The crown in the stone is the chapter's central image. Return to it at least three times after it is planted.
- Final spread: Egris walking away from everything, one soul choosing to follow — this is the emotional core of the series, established in the first chapter. Do not rush it.
